# Content Distribution and Repurposing

## What this skill does

Fixes the publish-and-pray problem: production is only half the job, and this skill builds the other half into the workflow.

## Inputs to collect

- Flagship content pipeline
- Channel list with owners (email, social, partners, community)
- Past performance data by channel

## Workflow

1. **Create the distribution pack at publish** - For each flagship piece: 3-5 social posts in the author's voice, an email segment draft, community post, and partner-share one-liner - made while the content is fresh in mind.
2. **Sequence the push** - Day 0 owned channels, day 2-3 community/partners, week 2 a second angle. One post is not distribution.
3. **Repurpose by format shift** - Article to thread/carousel, data point to visual, webinar to clips, case study to proof snippets. Change the format, not just the caption.
4. **Resurface winners** - Quarterly: top pieces get refreshed (per the refresh process) and re-promoted. Libraries compound when winners are re-run deliberately.
5. **Track per-piece reach** - Log distribution actions per piece so performance analysis can separate 'bad content' from 'never distributed'.

## Deliverable

- Distribution pack template and per-piece packs
- Distribution calendar layered on publishing
- Quarterly resurface list

## Success metrics

| Metric | How to read it |
|--------|---------------|
| Distribution actions per flagship piece | Target: every piece gets the full pack |
| Traffic per piece at 90 days | Distributed pieces vs historical baseline |
| Repurposed asset output per source piece | Efficiency of the content engine |

## Quality checklist

- [ ] Distribution pack created at publish time, not later
- [ ] Repurposing changes format, not just crops text
- [ ] Partner shares use assets partners can actually post

## Guardrails

- Claims discipline: use only proof the user supplies or verifies. Never invent clients, results, rankings, or testimonials.
- Privacy and consent: collect and use personal data only with a lawful basis and consent where required. Respect PDPA (Singapore) and GDPR; honour unsubscribes and opt-outs immediately.
- Platform policy: follow each ad and search platform's current policies. When a rule changes often, flag it for a human to re-check before launch.
- Original or properly licensed content only. No plagiarism, no scraped articles, no AI-spun rewrites of competitors' work.
- Statistics and quotes must trace to a real source the reader can check.
