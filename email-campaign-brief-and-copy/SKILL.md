# Email Campaign Brief and Copy

## What this skill does

Gives campaigns the discipline flows have by default: one job per send, written against a brief, with the segment and success metric fixed before copy starts.

## Inputs to collect

- Campaign goal (sell, educate, reactivate, announce) and the single CTA
- Segment definition and the insight about them right now
- Offer facts, dates and any compliance constraints

## Workflow

1. **Write the one-page brief** - Goal, audience, insight, message, proof, CTA, metric. If the brief has two goals, split the send.
2. **Draft subject and preview as a pair** - 5+ options; the pair must promise exactly what the email delivers. Curiosity is fine; deception is a deliverability and trust debt.
3. **Write body copy in layers** - Hook tied to the insight, substance (teach or show), proof, one CTA repeated once. Design for skimmers: the bolded lines alone should carry the message.
4. **Set the send mechanics** - Segment inclusions/exclusions (recent buyers, complainers, the newly welcomed), send time by audience behaviour, and the resend-to-non-openers rule - with a changed subject, after 48h+, max once.
5. **QA like it matters** - Links, personalisation fallbacks, mobile rendering, plain-text version, unsubscribe and address blocks. Send the test to a human, not just the ESP preview.

## Deliverable

- Campaign brief (one page)
- Final copy: subject options, body, CTA, plain-text version
- Send mechanics sheet and QA sign-off

## Success metrics

| Metric | How to read it |
|--------|---------------|
| Click rate (not just opens) | Apple MPP makes opens a soft metric; clicks are behaviour |
| Conversion per 1,000 sent | The campaign's business result |
| Unsubscribe rate per campaign | Above your list's normal band means the brief was wrong, not the audience |

## Quality checklist

- [ ] One goal, one CTA per campaign
- [ ] Subject line promise kept by the body copy
- [ ] Exclusions applied (buyers, complainers, in-flow conflicts)

## Guardrails

- Claims discipline: use only proof the user supplies or verifies. Never invent clients, results, rankings, or testimonials.
- Privacy and consent: collect and use personal data only with a lawful basis and consent where required. Respect PDPA (Singapore) and GDPR; honour unsubscribes and opt-outs immediately.
- Platform policy: follow each ad and search platform's current policies. When a rule changes often, flag it for a human to re-check before launch.
- Permission-based sending only: no purchased or scraped lists, ever. Clear consent, honest sender identity, one-click unsubscribe in every email.
- Deliverability is trust: no deceptive subject lines, no hidden downsides, no dark patterns in preference or unsubscribe flows.
