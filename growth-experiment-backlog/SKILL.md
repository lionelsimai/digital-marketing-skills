# Growth Experiment Backlog

## What this skill does

Creates the raw material of a growth programme: a backlog where every idea is a testable hypothesis tied to a funnel stage, not a suggestion box.

## Inputs to collect

- Funnel and cohort data showing the weakest stages
- User insight sources: interviews, support themes, session evidence
- Team capacity for experiments per cycle

## Workflow

1. **Source ideas from evidence** - Mine the drop-off diagnosis, cohort predictors and customer language. Ideas must cite their evidence; opinions enter at the bottom of the pile.
2. **Convert ideas to hypotheses** - Standard format: because [evidence], doing [change] for [segment] should move [metric] by [enough to matter]. Ideas that can't be converted get parked, not queued.
3. **Tag by stage and lever** - Acquisition, activation, retention, referral, revenue - plus the lever type (copy, offer, product, channel). The tags expose backlog imbalance (40 activation ideas, zero retention).
4. **Estimate cost honestly** - Design, dev, copy and analysis time per idea, in ranges. Cheap-looking tests that need a rebuild are how backlogs lie.
5. **Review and prune monthly** - Kill stale ideas whose evidence expired, merge duplicates, and re-source from fresh data. A backlog is a garden, not an archive.

## Deliverable

- Backlog with hypotheses, stages, evidence links
- Cost estimates per experiment
- Monthly pruning record

## Success metrics

| Metric | How to read it |
|--------|---------------|
| Share of backlog items with cited evidence | Target: all of the top 20 |
| Backlog balance by funnel stage | No stage starved for two consecutive cycles |
| Idea-to-test conversion | Share of prioritised ideas actually run per quarter |

## Quality checklist

- [ ] Every top idea carries its evidence
- [ ] Costs include analysis time, not just build time
- [ ] Pruning happens on schedule

## Guardrails

- Claims discipline: use only proof the user supplies or verifies. Never invent clients, results, rankings, or testimonials.
- Privacy and consent: collect and use personal data only with a lawful basis and consent where required. Respect PDPA (Singapore) and GDPR; honour unsubscribes and opt-outs immediately.
- Platform policy: follow each ad and search platform's current policies. When a rule changes often, flag it for a human to re-check before launch.
- Growth never overrides consent or honesty: no spam invites, no contact scraping for referral mechanics, no dark-pattern virality.
- Experiments on pricing and billing must be transparent to affected users - no silent price tests on existing customers' renewals.
