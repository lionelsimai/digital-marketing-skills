# Media Buying Negotiation Brief

## What this skill does

Stops media being bought on rate cards and relationships alone: a written brief of what you need, what you'll trade, and what you will not accept.

## Inputs to collect

- Planned spend and flight dates
- Rate history and competing quotes
- Measurement and cancellation requirements

## Workflow

1. **Define the buy objectives** - Audience, placements, context quality and outcomes - in priority order. A negotiation without ranked objectives defaults to price only.
2. **Set walk-away points** - Minimum guarantees (audience delivery, placement quality, make-good terms) below which you buy elsewhere or not at all.
3. **List tradeable value** - What you can offer beyond cash: longer commitment, case-study rights, first-party data insights, faster payment - each with an owner who can approve it.
4. **Demand measurement rights** - Third-party verification, log-level or placement-level reporting, and post-campaign analysis access written into the agreement.
5. **Document the deal** - Final terms, guarantees, make-good triggers and cancellation clauses in one summary both sides sign. Verbal deals are not deals.

## Deliverable

- Negotiation brief: objectives, walk-aways, tradeables
- Deal summary template with guarantee terms
- Post-buy delivery tracking sheet

## Success metrics

| Metric | How to read it |
|--------|---------------|
| Delivery vs guaranteed audience/placements | Held against the signed summary |
| Effective CPM vs rate card | Track the real discount, including value-adds |
| Make-goods claimed when owed | Guarantees unused are money left behind |

## Quality checklist

- [ ] Walk-away points approved by the budget owner before negotiating
- [ ] Measurement rights in writing
- [ ] No side agreements outside the documented deal

## Guardrails

- Claims discipline: use only proof the user supplies or verifies. Never invent clients, results, rankings, or testimonials.
- Privacy and consent: collect and use personal data only with a lawful basis and consent where required. Respect PDPA (Singapore) and GDPR; honour unsubscribes and opt-outs immediately.
- Platform policy: follow each ad and search platform's current policies. When a rule changes often, flag it for a human to re-check before launch.
- Buy media the business can measure. If a placement cannot be tracked or brand-lift measured, size it as an experiment, not a pillar.
