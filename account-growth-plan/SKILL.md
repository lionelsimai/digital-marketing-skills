# Account Growth Plan (Agency)

## What this skill does

Plans account growth from the client's results outward: where their business can win next, what it needs, and how the agency earns the right to deliver it.

## Inputs to collect

- Client's results and current scope performance
- Client's business roadmap and upcoming moments (launches, seasons, funding)
- Unmet needs observed during delivery (with evidence)

## Workflow

1. **Start from their scoreboard** - List what the current engagement has provably delivered. Expansion pitched on top of undelivered basics is how accounts churn.
2. **Map their next outcomes** - From their roadmap: the next business outcomes they'll need marketing for - new market, new product, new segment - with timing.
3. **Match needs to services honestly** - For each need: can we deliver this excellently, adequately, or not at all? 'Not at all' gets a partner recommendation, which buys more trust than a stretched yes.
4. **Sequence by their calendar** - Proposals timed to their planning cycles and moments, sized to the scope format that worked before. No end-of-quarter pressure tactics dressed as strategy.
5. **Track the relationship health signals** - Responsiveness, payment behaviour, stakeholder changes, results trend. Growth plans for unhealthy accounts start with a repair plan instead.

## Deliverable

- Account growth map: outcomes, matched services, timing
- Honesty column: deliver excellently / adequately / refer out
- Relationship health snapshot with repair items if needed

## Success metrics

| Metric | How to read it |
|--------|---------------|
| Expansion revenue from delivered-results accounts | Growth earned, not extracted |
| Client retention rate | The growth plan's foundation metric |
| Refer-out goodwill | Introductions made where the agency wasn't the right provider - tracked, because they return |

## Quality checklist

- [ ] Current scope's results verified before any expansion pitch
- [ ] At least one honest 'not us' where applicable
- [ ] Timing follows the client's calendar, not the agency's quarter

## Guardrails

- Claims discipline: use only proof the user supplies or verifies. Never invent clients, results, rankings, or testimonials.
- Privacy and consent: collect and use personal data only with a lawful basis and consent where required. Respect PDPA (Singapore) and GDPR; honour unsubscribes and opt-outs immediately.
- Platform policy: follow each ad and search platform's current policies. When a rule changes often, flag it for a human to re-check before launch.
- Client data stays confidential and is used only for that client's work. Never reuse one client's data, results or creative for another client's pitch without written permission.
- Report client results honestly, including the misses. A client who discovers a hidden failure leaves; a client told early usually stays.
