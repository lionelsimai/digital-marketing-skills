# Offer and Value Proposition Design

## What this skill does

Sharpens what is being sold before any copy is written, so pages, ads and emails inherit a clear promise instead of inventing one each time.

## Inputs to collect

- Product/service details and delivery process
- Verified proof: results, credentials, client logos (with permission)
- Price, terms and any guarantee the business can honestly offer

## Workflow

1. **Name the outcome** - Write the transformation in the buyer's words: from what painful state, to what desired state, in what timeframe they can verify.
2. **Explain the mechanism** - State how it works in 3-5 steps. A named, concrete mechanism is what separates this offer from generic alternatives.
3. **Stack the proof** - Attach the strongest verified proof to each claim: numbers, names, credentials, demonstrations. Remove claims with no proof behind them.
4. **Handle risk and price** - Add honest risk reversal (trial, guarantee, pilot) the business can fulfil, and frame price against the cost of the problem.
5. **Write the one-line proposition** - Compress to one sentence a stranger can repeat: who, outcome, mechanism, proof hint. Test it on the homepage headline and ad primary text.

## Deliverable

- Offer sheet: outcome, mechanism, proof, risk reversal, price framing
- One-line value proposition plus 3 headline variants
- Claims register: every claim and its proof source

## Success metrics

| Metric | How to read it |
|--------|---------------|
| Landing conversion rate after offer rewrite | The offer's report card |
| Sales call quality | Share of calls where the prospect repeats the proposition back accurately |
| Refund/guarantee claim rate | Risk reversal must be strong but sustainable |

## Quality checklist

- [ ] Every claim has a proof source in the claims register
- [ ] Guarantee terms are ones operations can actually honour
- [ ] Proposition understandable without industry jargon

## Guardrails

- Claims discipline: use only proof the user supplies or verifies. Never invent clients, results, rankings, or testimonials.
- Privacy and consent: collect and use personal data only with a lawful basis and consent where required. Respect PDPA (Singapore) and GDPR; honour unsubscribes and opt-outs immediately.
- Platform policy: follow each ad and search platform's current policies. When a rule changes often, flag it for a human to re-check before launch.
- Strategy before channels: never recommend a channel or budget split without the offer, audience and unit economics in hand.
