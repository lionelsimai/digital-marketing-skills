# Digital Marketing Audit Delivery

## What this skill does

Packages the audit as a decision document: what is broken, what it costs, what to fix first - credible because it also says what is working.

## Inputs to collect

- Audit access: analytics, ad accounts, site, CRM
- Client's goals and the scope they asked the audit to cover
- Benchmarks relevant to their market and size

## Workflow

1. **Audit against their goals** - Every section (tracking, site/CRO, search, paid, email, social proof) assessed for its effect on the client's stated goals - not against a generic 200-point checklist theatre.
2. **Grade findings honestly** - Critical (losing money now), important (capping growth), nice-to-have. Include a 'working well - protect this' section; audits that find only faults are sales documents, and clients can tell.
3. **Quantify the stakes** - Where data allows: the wasted spend, the conversion gap's revenue value, the tracking error's size. Ranges with stated assumptions beat false precision.
4. **Sequence the roadmap** - Quick wins (under 2 weeks), foundations (month 1-2), growth programmes (quarter). Each item with owner options: agency, client team, or hybrid.
5. **Present with the decision ask** - Walk through findings live, end with the specific next step being proposed and its price/scope. The audit's job is a clear yes or no, not applause.

## Deliverable

- Audit document: findings graded, stakes quantified, roadmap sequenced
- Quick-wins list executable immediately
- Proposal link: roadmap items mapped to scoped services

## Success metrics

| Metric | How to read it |
|--------|---------------|
| Audit-to-engagement conversion | The audit's commercial job - tracked without letting it corrupt honesty |
| Quick-win delivery rate | Promised quick wins shipped in the first month |
| Findings accuracy at 90 days | Sampled: did the critical findings prove real? |

## Quality checklist

- [ ] At least one genuine strength documented
- [ ] Every critical finding evidenced with data or screenshots
- [ ] Roadmap items have owner options, not agency-only assumptions

## Guardrails

- Claims discipline: use only proof the user supplies or verifies. Never invent clients, results, rankings, or testimonials.
- Privacy and consent: collect and use personal data only with a lawful basis and consent where required. Respect PDPA (Singapore) and GDPR; honour unsubscribes and opt-outs immediately.
- Platform policy: follow each ad and search platform's current policies. When a rule changes often, flag it for a human to re-check before launch.
- Client data stays confidential and is used only for that client's work. Never reuse one client's data, results or creative for another client's pitch without written permission.
- Report client results honestly, including the misses. A client who discovers a hidden failure leaves; a client told early usually stays.
