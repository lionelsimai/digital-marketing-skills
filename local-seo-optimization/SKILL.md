# Local SEO Optimization

## What this skill does

Builds a complete, accurate local presence for each location or service area so the business shows up - correctly - when nearby customers search.

## Inputs to collect

- Location list with addresses, hours, services per location
- Google Business Profile access
- Existing citations and review profiles

## Workflow

1. **Fix the profile fundamentals** - Primary category chosen for the main money service, complete attributes, real photos, accurate hours including holidays, and services listed per location.
2. **Make location pages earn their place** - One page per location with genuinely local content: team, service specifics, area served, directions, local proof. No doorway duplicates with swapped city names.
3. **Clean the citations** - Audit major directories for name/address/phone consistency. Fix the worst mismatches first; consistency beats quantity.
4. **Run a review engine** - Ask every happy customer at the right moment, make it one tap, and respond to every review - good and bad - within 2 business days.
5. **Add local proof content** - Publish location-relevant content: local projects, community involvement, area-specific FAQs drawn from real customer questions.

## Deliverable

- Per-location profile completion checklist
- Citation cleanup list ordered by mismatch severity
- Review request and response workflow

## Success metrics

| Metric | How to read it |
|--------|---------------|
| Profile actions (calls, directions, website clicks) | Per location, monthly from GBP insights |
| Map pack presence for core terms | Tracked per location and service |
| Review volume and rating trend | Velocity matters more than a perfect score |

## Quality checklist

- [ ] No fake locations or keyword-stuffed business names
- [ ] Reviews are requested, never bought or gated
- [ ] Location pages contain real local substance

## Guardrails

- Claims discipline: use only proof the user supplies or verifies. Never invent clients, results, rankings, or testimonials.
- Privacy and consent: collect and use personal data only with a lawful basis and consent where required. Respect PDPA (Singapore) and GDPR; honour unsubscribes and opt-outs immediately.
- Platform policy: follow each ad and search platform's current policies. When a rule changes often, flag it for a human to re-check before launch.
- White-hat only: no purchased links, link schemes, PBNs, keyword stuffing, cloaking or scraped/spun content. Earn coverage and links with assets worth citing.
- Never create fake reviews, fake locations, or keyword-stuffed profile names - all are suspension risks.
