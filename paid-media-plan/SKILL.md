# Paid Media Plan

## What this skill does

One plan that ties every paid line to a funnel job, a measurable outcome and a budget logic - so channels stop being bought in silos.

## Inputs to collect

- Campaign objective and period
- Audience definitions and creative assets available
- Historical channel benchmarks or test data

## Workflow

1. **Assign each line a job** - Capture, create or convert demand. Write the job and the primary metric on the plan itself, next to the budget.
2. **Build audiences per platform** - Translate personas into platform targeting: intent signals on search, interest/lookalike layers on social, context on display/video. Note exclusions.
3. **Set budgets and flighting** - Budget by expected marginal return, with flight dates around real business moments (paydays, seasons, launches), not calendar superstition.
4. **Specify creative per line** - Each line lists the exact creative it needs and who produces it by when. A plan without creative dependencies is fiction.
5. **Define measurement upfront** - Primary KPI, guardrail metric and decision rule per line: what result scales it, pauses it or sends it back for new creative.

## Deliverable

- Media plan: lines, jobs, audiences, budgets, flights
- Creative dependency list with owners and dates
- Decision rules per line (scale/pause/fix)

## Success metrics

| Metric | How to read it |
|--------|---------------|
| Marginal CPA by line | The next dollar's cost, not the average |
| Plan vs actual delivery | Spend pacing and audience reach per line |
| Creative readiness at launch | Lines launched with full creative vs partial |

## Quality checklist

- [ ] Every line has a job, a metric and a decision rule
- [ ] Creative dependencies have owners and dates
- [ ] Brand and performance lines judged by different criteria

## Guardrails

- Claims discipline: use only proof the user supplies or verifies. Never invent clients, results, rankings, or testimonials.
- Privacy and consent: collect and use personal data only with a lawful basis and consent where required. Respect PDPA (Singapore) and GDPR; honour unsubscribes and opt-outs immediately.
- Platform policy: follow each ad and search platform's current policies. When a rule changes often, flag it for a human to re-check before launch.
- Buy media the business can measure. If a placement cannot be tracked or brand-lift measured, size it as an experiment, not a pillar.
