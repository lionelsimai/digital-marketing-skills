# Freelancer and Subcontractor Briefing

## What this skill does

Extends the agency's quality bar beyond its payroll: external contributors get the same clarity as internal staff, plus the legal and confidentiality basics.

## Inputs to collect

- Deliverable spec and deadline
- Brand assets, guidelines and examples of the standard
- Client confidentiality and IP requirements for the engagement

## Workflow

1. **Select and contract first** - Written agreement before work: scope, fee, timeline, IP assignment on payment, confidentiality, and the rule that client identity/data isn't used for their portfolio without permission.
2. **Brief with context, not just tasks** - The client's goal, the audience, where this deliverable sits in the campaign, and what good looks like - with two examples (one to emulate, one to avoid, anonymised where needed).
3. **Specify the deliverable exactly** - Formats, sizes, naming conventions, source-file requirements, revision rounds included and the approval path. Ambiguity here becomes unpaid revision wars later.
4. **Set the communication protocol** - One channel, one agency contact, response expectations, and the escalation for blockers. Freelancers never contact the client directly unless explicitly arranged.
5. **Review, pay, and record** - Review against the spec (not taste), pay on the agreed terms promptly, and log performance for the roster: quality, speed, communication. Good rosters are built on records, not memory.

## Deliverable

- Briefing pack: context, spec, examples, protocol
- Signed agreement with IP and confidentiality terms
- Roster performance log entry

## Success metrics

| Metric | How to read it |
|--------|---------------|
| First-delivery acceptance rate | Brief quality, measured at the freelancer's first submission |
| Revision rounds per deliverable | Against the contracted allowance |
| On-time delivery rate | By contributor - feeds roster decisions |

## Quality checklist

- [ ] Agreement signed before work starts
- [ ] Client confidentiality terms flow down explicitly
- [ ] Deliverable spec includes source files and naming

## Guardrails

- Claims discipline: use only proof the user supplies or verifies. Never invent clients, results, rankings, or testimonials.
- Privacy and consent: collect and use personal data only with a lawful basis and consent where required. Respect PDPA (Singapore) and GDPR; honour unsubscribes and opt-outs immediately.
- Platform policy: follow each ad and search platform's current policies. When a rule changes often, flag it for a human to re-check before launch.
- Client data stays confidential and is used only for that client's work. Never reuse one client's data, results or creative for another client's pitch without written permission.
- Report client results honestly, including the misses. A client who discovers a hidden failure leaves; a client told early usually stays.
- Never share client data, credentials or customer information with subcontractors beyond what the task strictly needs - and never without the client's knowledge where their data is involved.
