# Long-Form Article Production

## What this skill does

A repeatable production workflow for flagship articles - the kind that rank, get cited and get sent around - without heroic one-off effort.

## Inputs to collect

- Approved brief (intent, angle, outline)
- Expert sources: interviews, internal data, primary documents
- Style guide and example pieces that set the bar

## Workflow

1. **Research before writing** - Read the top sources, gather primary evidence, and interview one real expert (internal counts). Note the specific facts only this research could produce.
2. **Draft to the outline** - Write section by section against the brief. Lead each section with its answer; depth follows clarity, never precedes it.
3. **Add the proof layer** - Insert data, examples, screenshots or worked calculations wherever a claim is made. Flag any claim that still lacks evidence for cutting or sourcing.
4. **Edit in two passes** - Pass 1 structure and accuracy (does it answer the intent, are facts right). Pass 2 line edit for clarity and voice. Different hats, different days if possible.
5. **Publish as a package** - Metadata, internal links per the cluster plan, distribution assets (social posts, email segment) prepared at publish - not a week later.

## Deliverable

- Published article with proof layer and links
- Research file: sources and interview notes
- Distribution asset set created at publish

## Success metrics

| Metric | How to read it |
|--------|---------------|
| Rankings and qualified traffic at 90 days | Long-form pays late; judge it late |
| Citations/backlinks earned | Flagship content should attract references |
| Time on page / scroll depth | Depth readers are the point of the format |

## Quality checklist

- [ ] Every statistic sourced and checkable
- [ ] Expert input is real, named where permission allows
- [ ] Article answers its brief's intent in the first screen

## Guardrails

- Claims discipline: use only proof the user supplies or verifies. Never invent clients, results, rankings, or testimonials.
- Privacy and consent: collect and use personal data only with a lawful basis and consent where required. Respect PDPA (Singapore) and GDPR; honour unsubscribes and opt-outs immediately.
- Platform policy: follow each ad and search platform's current policies. When a rule changes often, flag it for a human to re-check before launch.
- Original or properly licensed content only. No plagiarism, no scraped articles, no AI-spun rewrites of competitors' work.
- Statistics and quotes must trace to a real source the reader can check.
