# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-11-09

Initial release: 120 digital marketing skills across 12 categories.

- **Digital Strategy & Funnels** (10) — strategy plans, funnel architecture, personas, journey mapping, budget allocation, channel mix, campaign calendars, competitor audits, offer design, growth models
- **SEO** (10) — technical audits, keyword mapping, on-page, content briefs, internal linking, local SEO, link earning & digital PR, content refresh, schema markup, reporting
- **SEM & PPC** (10) — account structure, campaign builds, match-type strategy, search ad copy, bidding, Quality Score, negative keywords, message match, RLSA, account audits
- **Paid Media Planning & Buying** (10) — media plans, budget scenarios, paid social structure, programmatic, YouTube ads, negotiation, fatigue management, reallocation, incrementality & geo testing, reporting cadence
- **Content Marketing** (10) — strategy, editorial operations, pillar/cluster planning, long-form production, lead magnets, case studies, webinars, newsletters, distribution, performance reviews
- **Email & Lifecycle Marketing** (10) — flow architecture, welcome flows, abandon recovery, campaign copy, deliverability, list hygiene & segmentation, lifecycle mapping, winback, transactional email, programme reporting
- **Marketing Automation & CRM** (10) — lead scoring, nurture workflows, CRM hygiene, lifecycle stages, sales handoff & SLA, workflow audits, event automation, personalisation, consent & preference centres, martech review
- **CRO & Landing Pages** (10) — heuristic audits, A/B test design, landing copy, forms, headline/CTA testing, drop-off diagnosis, social proof, pricing pages, checkout review, results writeups
- **Web & Marketing Analytics** (10) — GA4 measurement plans, UTM governance, attribution comparison, dashboards, metric definitions, campaign analysis, cohort analysis, server-side tracking, consent mode, weekly scorecard
- **Brand & Creative** (10) — positioning, messaging frameworks, creative briefs, ad concepting, brand voice, visual identity briefs, message houses, creative testing matrices, proof libraries, rebrand rollouts
- **Growth Experiments & PLG** (10) — experiment backlogs, ICE/RICE prioritisation, activation audits, referral design, viral loop analysis, trial conversion, pricing experiments, growth loops, retention curves, programme operations
- **Agency & Client Delivery** (10) — discovery & onboarding, audit delivery, proposals & scope, retainer reporting, communication cadence, sprint planning, launch QA, account growth, freelancer briefing, monthly reviews

Includes CATALOG.md + catalog.json, a validator (`tools/validate.py`)
and the generator that renders skills from authored data
(`tools/build_skills.py`). MIT licensed.
