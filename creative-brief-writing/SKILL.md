# Creative Brief Writing

## What this skill does

The one-page contract between strategy and creative - tight enough to focus, open enough to let creative people surprise you.

## Inputs to collect

- Campaign objective and the decision it must drive
- Audience insight (evidence-based, specific)
- Mandatories: offer facts, brand rules, legal constraints, formats needed

## Workflow

1. **Write the single-minded proposition** - One sentence the whole execution must prove. If the brief has two propositions, it has none - choose.
2. **State the insight, not the demographic** - 'Ops managers who got burned by a failed rollout' beats 'IT decision-makers 35-54'. The insight is the tension the creative must resolve.
3. **List mandatories separately** - Facts, logos, legal lines, format specs - in a box marked non-negotiable, so creatives know exactly where the fence is and how much field they have.
4. **Define success and review criteria** - What the work must achieve and how concepts will be judged (proposition clarity, insight truth, brand fit, producibility) - agreed before concepts are shown.
5. **Brief in person, briefly** - Walk the creative team through it, take questions, and record answers in the brief. The document is the memory of that conversation.

## Deliverable

- One-page creative brief
- Review criteria agreed with stakeholders
- Q&A addendum from the briefing session

## Success metrics

| Metric | How to read it |
|--------|---------------|
| First-round concept acceptance rate | Brief quality shows up here |
| Rounds of revision per asset | Falling rounds = clearer briefs |
| Creative performance vs brief's proposition | Post-campaign: did the winning work prove the proposition? |

## Quality checklist

- [ ] Exactly one proposition
- [ ] Insight cites evidence, not adjectives about age bands
- [ ] Mandatories complete, including legal lines

## Guardrails

- Claims discipline: use only proof the user supplies or verifies. Never invent clients, results, rankings, or testimonials.
- Privacy and consent: collect and use personal data only with a lawful basis and consent where required. Respect PDPA (Singapore) and GDPR; honour unsubscribes and opt-outs immediately.
- Platform policy: follow each ad and search platform's current policies. When a rule changes often, flag it for a human to re-check before launch.
- Brand claims obey the same proof rules as performance claims. Aspiration is fine; fabrication is not.
