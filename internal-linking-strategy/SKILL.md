# Internal Linking Strategy

## What this skill does

Turns internal links from an afterthought into a system: priority pages get depth-3 reachability and descriptive anchors from relevant content.

## Inputs to collect

- URL inventory with traffic and revenue data
- Keyword map showing owner pages
- Crawl data with link depth and orphan list

## Workflow

1. **Rank pages by value** - Tier pages: tier 1 money pages, tier 2 supporting content, tier 3 archive. Linking effort follows the tiers.
2. **Build hub-and-spoke clusters** - Group content by topic cluster around each owner page. Every spoke links up to its hub; hubs link down and across to sibling hubs.
3. **Fix reachability** - Get every tier 1-2 page within 3 clicks of the homepage. Rescue orphans by linking from relevant live pages or retire them deliberately.
4. **Set anchor rules** - Descriptive, varied anchors that name the destination topic. Ban 'click here' and identical exact-match anchors repeated site-wide.
5. **Add links in editorial flow** - Produce a per-page list of link additions with source page, anchor and placement context for the content team.

## Deliverable

- Cluster map with hub/spoke assignments
- Link addition worklist (source, anchor, target)
- Orphan and depth report with dispositions

## Success metrics

| Metric | How to read it |
|--------|---------------|
| Average click depth of tier 1 pages | Target 3 or less |
| Orphan count | To zero for live content |
| Internal referral traffic to money pages | Visible in analytics after changes |

## Quality checklist

- [ ] Anchors describe the destination, not the click
- [ ] No link schemes - internal links must help a reader navigate
- [ ] Retired pages get redirects, not silent deletions

## Guardrails

- Claims discipline: use only proof the user supplies or verifies. Never invent clients, results, rankings, or testimonials.
- Privacy and consent: collect and use personal data only with a lawful basis and consent where required. Respect PDPA (Singapore) and GDPR; honour unsubscribes and opt-outs immediately.
- Platform policy: follow each ad and search platform's current policies. When a rule changes often, flag it for a human to re-check before launch.
- White-hat only: no purchased links, link schemes, PBNs, keyword stuffing, cloaking or scraped/spun content. Earn coverage and links with assets worth citing.
