# Experiment Programme Operations

## What this skill does

The management system that keeps experiments shipping and learnings compounding after the initial enthusiasm fades: rituals, records and standards.

## Inputs to collect

- Backlog and prioritisation framework in place
- Team roles: who designs, builds, analyses, decides
- Current experiment log state (or its absence)

## Workflow

1. **Set the operating cadence** - Weekly: pipeline review (what launches, what's blocked). Fortnightly: results review (decisions taken, not just readouts). Monthly: programme health (velocity, win rate, learning themes).
2. **Standardise the artefacts** - One template each for test design, QA record and results writeup - short enough to complete, complete enough to trust. Templates live where the work happens.
3. **Maintain the single log** - Every experiment - planned, running, done, killed - in one searchable log with status and links. If it isn't in the log, it didn't happen; if it's only in the log's owner's head, it will be re-run.
4. **Guard the rigour gates** - No launch without sizing and a pre-registered rule; no result without a writeup. The programme lead enforces gates kindly and absolutely - rigour is the brand.
5. **Publish the learning digest** - Quarterly digest of what the programme learned, organised by theme, sent to product, marketing and leadership. Learning that stays in the growth team is only half-earned.

## Deliverable

- Programme operating manual: cadence, templates, gates
- Single experiment log (live system)
- Quarterly learning digest format

## Success metrics

| Metric | How to read it |
|--------|---------------|
| Experiment velocity (launched per cycle) | Balanced against the rigour metrics below |
| Gate compliance rate | Share of launches with sizing + pre-registration on file |
| Writeup completion and reuse | Results filed and cited by later designs |

## Quality checklist

- [ ] Cadence meetings have fixed agendas and owners
- [ ] Log is complete - sampled against shipped changes
- [ ] Gates enforced on 100% of launches, including leadership requests

## Guardrails

- Claims discipline: use only proof the user supplies or verifies. Never invent clients, results, rankings, or testimonials.
- Privacy and consent: collect and use personal data only with a lawful basis and consent where required. Respect PDPA (Singapore) and GDPR; honour unsubscribes and opt-outs immediately.
- Platform policy: follow each ad and search platform's current policies. When a rule changes often, flag it for a human to re-check before launch.
- Growth never overrides consent or honesty: no spam invites, no contact scraping for referral mechanics, no dark-pattern virality.
- Experiments on pricing and billing must be transparent to affected users - no silent price tests on existing customers' renewals.
