# Case Study Production

## What this skill does

Turns a happy client into the business's strongest proof asset - with a process that protects the relationship and the truth of the numbers.

## Inputs to collect

- Candidate client with real, checkable results
- Client permission process and any legal/brand constraints
- Baseline and outcome data from delivery records

## Workflow

1. **Select and secure permission** - Choose clients whose results are typical enough to be honest and strong enough to matter. Get written permission for names, logos and numbers before drafting.
2. **Interview for the story** - 30-45 minutes: the problem before, why they chose you, what the work looked like, the moment it clicked, the results, what they'd tell a peer.
3. **Verify every number** - Cross-check claimed results against delivery/analytics records. Agree the exact figures and timeframes with the client in writing.
4. **Write the narrative** - Situation, stakes, decision, work, results, quote. Specific beats emotional; numbers beat adjectives.
5. **Produce the format family** - Full web page, one-page PDF for sales, 3 social proof snippets, and the slide version. One production, many uses.

## Deliverable

- Published case study page with approved quotes and figures
- One-page sales PDF and snippet set
- Permission record filed with the asset

## Quality checklist

- [ ] Written permission on file before publication
- [ ] Every figure verified against records and agreed with the client
- [ ] Results presented with timeframe and context, not cherry-picked fragments

## Guardrails

- Claims discipline: use only proof the user supplies or verifies. Never invent clients, results, rankings, or testimonials.
- Privacy and consent: collect and use personal data only with a lawful basis and consent where required. Respect PDPA (Singapore) and GDPR; honour unsubscribes and opt-outs immediately.
- Platform policy: follow each ad and search platform's current policies. When a rule changes often, flag it for a human to re-check before launch.
- Original or properly licensed content only. No plagiarism, no scraped articles, no AI-spun rewrites of competitors' work.
- Statistics and quotes must trace to a real source the reader can check.
- Never publish a client's name, logo or results without explicit permission. Never round a result into a different claim.
