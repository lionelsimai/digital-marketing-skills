# CRO Audit (Heuristic)

## What this skill does

A fast, structured page review that finds conversion leaks before any testing starts, with each issue tied to evidence and a proposed fix.

## Inputs to collect

- Pages in scope with their conversion goals
- Analytics: traffic, conversion, device split per page
- Session recordings or heatmaps if available

## Workflow

1. **Score the five lenses** - Rate each page on Clarity (5-second comprehension), Value (why act), Friction (effort to complete), Anxiety (trust gaps), Distraction (competing exits). Score 1-5 with screenshots as evidence.
2. **Check the message chain** - Trace ad/search promise to headline to CTA. Breaks in the chain are the highest-yield findings in most audits.
3. **Inspect the mechanics** - Load speed on mobile, form behaviour, error states, tap targets, and what happens after conversion (dead-end thank-you pages are wasted assets).
4. **Mine the voice of visitor** - Support tickets, chat logs, on-site survey answers about these pages. Visitors describe the friction better than any framework.
5. **Prioritise the findings** - Rank by expected impact x traffic x ease. Deliver the top 10 with fix descriptions a designer/dev can action without a meeting.

## Deliverable

- Audit scorecard per page with evidence screenshots
- Top-10 fix list in priority order
- Test candidates list for issues that need data, not opinion

## Success metrics

| Metric | How to read it |
|--------|---------------|
| Audit issues fixed within 30 days | Audits that sit in decks change nothing |
| Conversion change on fixed pages | Measured against matched pre-period |
| Repeat-issue rate in the next audit | Same findings twice means the process, not the page, is broken |

## Quality checklist

- [ ] Every finding has visual evidence
- [ ] Fixes separated from test hypotheses
- [ ] Mobile experience audited on real rendering, not desktop shrink

## Guardrails

- Claims discipline: use only proof the user supplies or verifies. Never invent clients, results, rankings, or testimonials.
- Privacy and consent: collect and use personal data only with a lawful basis and consent where required. Respect PDPA (Singapore) and GDPR; honour unsubscribes and opt-outs immediately.
- Platform policy: follow each ad and search platform's current policies. When a rule changes often, flag it for a human to re-check before launch.
- No dark patterns: no fake countdowns, confirm-shaming, hidden costs or pre-ticked add-ons. Conversion bought with deception is refunded with interest in trust.
- Social proof must be real: genuine testimonials with permission, accurate numbers, no fabricated reviews or logos.
