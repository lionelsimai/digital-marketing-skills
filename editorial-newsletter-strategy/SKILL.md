# Editorial Newsletter Strategy

## What this skill does

Treats the newsletter as a media product with a reader promise - not a company bulletin - and builds the growth and retention loops around it.

## Inputs to collect

- Target reader and the one job the newsletter does for them
- Writer voice and available production time
- Current list size, open/click baselines (if any)

## Workflow

1. **Write the reader promise** - One sentence: who it's for, what they get, how often, and why this sender can deliver it. If the promise is 'updates from us', start over.
2. **Fix a repeatable format** - Named sections in a stable order, one lead piece of substance per issue, scannable in 3-5 minutes. Consistency is what makes opens habitual.
3. **Set a sustainable cadence** - Weekly or fortnightly at a quality the writer can hold for a year. Missed issues cost more trust than a slower cadence.
4. **Build growth loops** - Signup points on highest-traffic pages, lead magnet alignment, cross-promotions with aligned newsletters, and a referral mechanic once the base is loyal.
5. **Review like a product** - Monthly: opens, clicks, replies, unsubscribes and reader replies quoted verbatim. Replies are the loyalty metric vanity metrics hide.

## Deliverable

- Newsletter charter: promise, format, cadence, voice
- Growth loop plan with signup placements
- Monthly review template

## Success metrics

| Metric | How to read it |
|--------|---------------|
| Open and click rates vs own baseline | Judge against your list's history, not industry folklore |
| Reply rate | Readers who write back are an asset competitors can't copy |
| Net list growth and unsubscribe rate | Growth that churns is a leaky bucket |

## Quality checklist

- [ ] Promise written from the reader's side
- [ ] Cadence provably sustainable for the writer
- [ ] Every issue contains substance, not only self-promotion

## Guardrails

- Claims discipline: use only proof the user supplies or verifies. Never invent clients, results, rankings, or testimonials.
- Privacy and consent: collect and use personal data only with a lawful basis and consent where required. Respect PDPA (Singapore) and GDPR; honour unsubscribes and opt-outs immediately.
- Platform policy: follow each ad and search platform's current policies. When a rule changes often, flag it for a human to re-check before launch.
- Original or properly licensed content only. No plagiarism, no scraped articles, no AI-spun rewrites of competitors' work.
- Statistics and quotes must trace to a real source the reader can check.
