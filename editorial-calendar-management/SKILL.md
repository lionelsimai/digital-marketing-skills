# Editorial Calendar Management

## What this skill does

Turns content intentions into a production system with visible states (idea, briefed, drafting, review, scheduled) and honest capacity limits.

## Inputs to collect

- Content pillars and priority topics
- Team: writers, reviewers, approvers and their real availability
- Publishing channels and cadence targets

## Workflow

1. **Set cadence from capacity** - Count real production hours, including review cycles. Two finished pieces beat five stuck in review.
2. **Build the pipeline board** - One board, five states, every piece visible with owner and due date. Ideas live in a separate backlog, not on the calendar.
3. **Brief before drafting** - No draft starts without a brief (intent, angle, proof, CTA). The brief is the contract that makes review fast.
4. **Protect review SLAs** - Reviewers get deadlines too (e.g. 2 business days). Content dies in inboxes more often than in drafting.
5. **Weekly production standup** - 15 minutes: what's stuck, what's shipping, what gets killed. Killing zombie pieces is a feature.

## Deliverable

- Editorial board with states and SLAs
- Cadence plan tied to capacity
- Brief template adopted for all pieces

## Success metrics

| Metric | How to read it |
|--------|---------------|
| On-time publication rate | The calendar's core promise |
| Average days brief-to-publish | Find where time actually goes |
| Zombie piece count | Pieces stuck 30+ days; target near zero |

## Quality checklist

- [ ] Cadence derived from real capacity, not ambition
- [ ] Review deadlines exist and are enforced kindly
- [ ] Backlog separated from committed calendar

## Guardrails

- Claims discipline: use only proof the user supplies or verifies. Never invent clients, results, rankings, or testimonials.
- Privacy and consent: collect and use personal data only with a lawful basis and consent where required. Respect PDPA (Singapore) and GDPR; honour unsubscribes and opt-outs immediately.
- Platform policy: follow each ad and search platform's current policies. When a rule changes often, flag it for a human to re-check before launch.
- Original or properly licensed content only. No plagiarism, no scraped articles, no AI-spun rewrites of competitors' work.
- Statistics and quotes must trace to a real source the reader can check.
