# PPC Landing Page Alignment

## What this skill does

Closes the gap between what the ad promised and what the page delivers - the cheapest conversion lift in most paid search accounts.

## Inputs to collect

- Ad group themes with their ad copy
- Current landing pages and their conversion rates
- Offer details and proof per theme

## Workflow

1. **Score the match** - For each top ad group: does the page H1 mirror the ad promise? Does the page answer this theme's specific question, or a generic one? Score 1-5.
2. **Fix the first screen** - Headline = ad promise in page words, subhead = proof or mechanism, CTA visible without scrolling on mobile. Nothing else matters if this fails.
3. **Deepen per theme** - Add the theme-specific section: the exact service variant, use case or audience the ad group targets. Generic pages get a specific section, not a clone farm.
4. **One action per page** - Remove competing CTAs. Secondary actions (call, chat) support the primary one; they don't fight it.
5. **Verify tracking on the action** - Test the conversion end to end from a clicked ad (with test parameters) through to the CRM or thank-you event.

## Deliverable

- Message-match scorecard per top ad group
- Page fix list ordered by spend behind the page
- Tracking verification record per conversion action

## Success metrics

| Metric | How to read it |
|--------|---------------|
| Landing conversion rate by ad group | The alignment dividend shows here |
| Bounce/engaged rate from paid search | Mismatch shows as instant exits |
| Cost per conversion on fixed themes | Compare 30 days before/after at similar volume |

## Quality checklist

- [ ] Page headline could be swapped with the ad promise without a reader noticing a seam
- [ ] No page promises what the ad didn't - and vice versa
- [ ] Mobile first screen checked on a real device

## Guardrails

- Claims discipline: use only proof the user supplies or verifies. Never invent clients, results, rankings, or testimonials.
- Privacy and consent: collect and use personal data only with a lawful basis and consent where required. Respect PDPA (Singapore) and GDPR; honour unsubscribes and opt-outs immediately.
- Platform policy: follow each ad and search platform's current policies. When a rule changes often, flag it for a human to re-check before launch.
- Ad policy compliance: no misleading claims, no prohibited content, no trademark abuse. Ads must promise only what the landing page delivers.
- Never advertise results, prices or offers the business cannot honour today.
