# Negative Keyword Management

## What this skill does

Stops budget leaking to irrelevant searches while protecting good traffic - negatives are precision work, not a weekly dump of every odd term.

## Inputs to collect

- Search term reports (30-90 days) with cost and conversions
- Offer definition: what the business does NOT sell or serve
- Existing negative lists and their history

## Workflow

1. **Mine the waste** - Sort terms by cost with zero conversions and by irrelevance. Separate 'wrong intent' from 'right intent, hasn't converted yet' - different handling.
2. **Decide match type per negative** - Exact for single bad terms, phrase for bad themes (jobs, free, DIY where inappropriate), broad only with extreme care and senior review.
3. **Build shared lists by theme** - Account-level lists: wrong-industry, employment, education/research intent, free-seekers, competitor-irrelevant. Apply by tier with documented exceptions.
4. **Check false positives** - Before adding, search your converting terms for overlap. A negative that blocks a money query is worse than the waste it prevents.
5. **Set the cadence** - Weekly mining for active accounts, monthly list review, and a quarterly audit that removes stale negatives blocking new products.

## Deliverable

- Themed negative lists with application map
- Mining log: terms reviewed, decision, reason
- Cadence and ownership for ongoing management

## Success metrics

| Metric | How to read it |
|--------|---------------|
| Spend on irrelevant terms | Should fall month on month after cleanup |
| Wasted-click ratio | Clicks on terms with no path to the offer |
| Blocked-good-term incidents | Target zero; audit trail catches them |

## Quality checklist

- [ ] False-positive check done for every phrase/broad negative
- [ ] Negatives never added from one impression of data
- [ ] Lists documented so a new operator understands each theme

## Guardrails

- Claims discipline: use only proof the user supplies or verifies. Never invent clients, results, rankings, or testimonials.
- Privacy and consent: collect and use personal data only with a lawful basis and consent where required. Respect PDPA (Singapore) and GDPR; honour unsubscribes and opt-outs immediately.
- Platform policy: follow each ad and search platform's current policies. When a rule changes often, flag it for a human to re-check before launch.
- Ad policy compliance: no misleading claims, no prohibited content, no trademark abuse. Ads must promise only what the landing page delivers.
- Never advertise results, prices or offers the business cannot honour today.
