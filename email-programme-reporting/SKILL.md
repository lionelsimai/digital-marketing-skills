# Email Programme Reporting

## What this skill does

A monthly report that answers 'is email earning and healthy' in business terms, with the vanity metrics demoted to diagnostics.

## Inputs to collect

- ESP reports: flows, campaigns, revenue attribution
- List growth, churn and deliverability data
- Experiment log for the period

## Workflow

1. **Lead with economics** - Revenue per subscriber per month, flow vs campaign revenue split, and cost (tooling + time) beside it. Email should be able to show a P&L.
2. **Report health next** - Deliverability, complaint/unsubscribe trends, engaged share of list, and sunset actions taken. Health leads revenue by a quarter.
3. **Grade the experiments** - Each test: hypothesis, result, decision. Programme learning compounds only when decisions are recorded.
4. **Diagnose the biggest mover** - Pick the largest revenue or health movement and explain it with evidence - segment, flow or campaign level, not 'engagement was up'.
5. **Commit next month's work** - Three actions max: one revenue, one health, one experiment. With owners and expected effects.

## Deliverable

- Monthly email P&L-style report
- Experiment decision log update
- Next month's 3 committed actions

## Success metrics

| Metric | How to read it |
|--------|---------------|
| Revenue per subscriber per month | The programme's headline |
| Flow revenue share | Healthy programmes grow the automated share over time |
| Engaged list share trend | The leading indicator for next quarter |

## Quality checklist

- [ ] Opens reported as directional only (MPP caveat stated)
- [ ] Attribution method named and consistent month to month
- [ ] Health metrics never omitted in a good revenue month

## Guardrails

- Claims discipline: use only proof the user supplies or verifies. Never invent clients, results, rankings, or testimonials.
- Privacy and consent: collect and use personal data only with a lawful basis and consent where required. Respect PDPA (Singapore) and GDPR; honour unsubscribes and opt-outs immediately.
- Platform policy: follow each ad and search platform's current policies. When a rule changes often, flag it for a human to re-check before launch.
- Permission-based sending only: no purchased or scraped lists, ever. Clear consent, honest sender identity, one-click unsubscribe in every email.
- Deliverability is trust: no deceptive subject lines, no hidden downsides, no dark patterns in preference or unsubscribe flows.
