# Welcome Flow Build

## What this skill does

The highest-leverage flow in most programmes: converts a fresh signup's peak attention into orientation, trust and a first action.

## Inputs to collect

- What the subscriber signed up for (lead magnet, newsletter, trial)
- The one first action that predicts long-term value
- Brand story assets and best existing content

## Workflow

1. **Email 0: deliver instantly** - The promised asset or confirmation within minutes, from a real person/name where possible, with one clear next step. Speed is the message.
2. **Set expectations and the story** - Email 2: what they'll receive, how often, and the credible story behind the sender - proof early, biography late.
3. **Teach the first win** - Email 3: guide the single first action that correlates with retention (use the tool, read the key guide, book the call), with friction removed.
4. **Segment by behaviour** - Branch on clicks/opens of specific links: interested in topic A vs B gets different next content and offers. Record the tags.
5. **Close with the offer or the habit** - Final email: the core offer with honest framing, or the habit loop (what to expect every week) for content lists. Then hand off cleanly to campaigns.

## Deliverable

- Welcome flow: copy, spacing, branch rules
- Tagging/segmentation map from flow behaviour
- Handoff rules into campaigns and other flows

## Success metrics

| Metric | How to read it |
|--------|---------------|
| Open/click rate on email 0 and 1 | Delivery and promise-match health |
| First-action completion rate | The flow's true objective |
| Welcome-to-customer conversion | Against a no-flow or old-flow baseline where possible |

## Quality checklist

- [ ] Delivery email arrives within minutes in testing
- [ ] Branches tested with seed accounts in each path
- [ ] Unsubscribe and preference links present in every email

## Guardrails

- Claims discipline: use only proof the user supplies or verifies. Never invent clients, results, rankings, or testimonials.
- Privacy and consent: collect and use personal data only with a lawful basis and consent where required. Respect PDPA (Singapore) and GDPR; honour unsubscribes and opt-outs immediately.
- Platform policy: follow each ad and search platform's current policies. When a rule changes often, flag it for a human to re-check before launch.
- Permission-based sending only: no purchased or scraped lists, ever. Clear consent, honest sender identity, one-click unsubscribe in every email.
- Deliverability is trust: no deceptive subject lines, no hidden downsides, no dark patterns in preference or unsubscribe flows.
