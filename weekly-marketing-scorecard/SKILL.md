# Weekly Marketing Scorecard

## What this skill does

A one-page weekly discipline that keeps the whole team reading the same reality: short, consistent, and finished with decisions - not a data dump.

## Inputs to collect

- House metrics dictionary and dashboard
- Targets and pacing (monthly/quarterly goals pro-rated)
- Change log: campaigns, tests, site changes this week

## Workflow

1. **Open with outcomes vs pace** - Revenue/pipeline/leads vs the pro-rated target, RAG status with the gap in numbers, not adjectives.
2. **Show the funnel's vital signs** - Stage conversion rates with week-on-week deltas; flag any stage outside its normal band - that's where the investigation section starts.
3. **Report spend efficiency** - Spend vs plan and blended CAC/CPL vs allowable, with the biggest channel mover named.
4. **Write 'what changed and what we learned'** - Three bullets max: launches, test results, external factors. If nothing was learned this week, say so - it's diagnostic.
5. **Close with decisions** - The 1-3 actions this scorecard triggers, with owners and dates. The scorecard exists to cause these.

## Deliverable

- One-page weekly scorecard in the fixed template
- Investigation notes for out-of-band metrics
- Decision list with owners

## Success metrics

| Metric | How to read it |
|--------|---------------|
| Scorecard published on time | Same day, same time - rhythm is the product |
| Decision completion from last week's card | Reviewed at the top of the next card |
| Weeks to detect a funnel break | Should shrink as bands get calibrated |

## Quality checklist

- [ ] Template identical week to week
- [ ] Every RAG status backed by a number
- [ ] Decisions from last week reviewed before new ones are set

## Guardrails

- Claims discipline: use only proof the user supplies or verifies. Never invent clients, results, rankings, or testimonials.
- Privacy and consent: collect and use personal data only with a lawful basis and consent where required. Respect PDPA (Singapore) and GDPR; honour unsubscribes and opt-outs immediately.
- Platform policy: follow each ad and search platform's current policies. When a rule changes often, flag it for a human to re-check before launch.
- Track only with consent where required: respect consent mode, PDPA/GDPR obligations and platform policies. No fingerprinting workarounds, no tracking people who said no.
- Report uncertainty honestly: modelled and sampled data labelled as such; never present platform attribution as ground truth.
