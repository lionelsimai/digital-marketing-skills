# Retainer Reporting Pack

## What this skill does

Turns reporting from a defensive data dump into the engagement's steering wheel: the client sees value, decisions get made, renewals get easier.

## Inputs to collect

- Baseline and current results across channels
- Work delivered log for the month
- Experiment/test results and next month's roadmap

## Workflow

1. **Lead with outcomes vs goals** - Page one: the 3-5 business metrics the engagement is judged on, vs baseline and vs target, with honest RAG status. Activity appears later, never first.
2. **Explain the movements** - For each major movement: what we did, what external factors did, and what's hypothesis. Clients don't fire agencies for bad months; they fire them for unexplained months.
3. **Show the work and the learning** - Deliverables shipped (against scope), tests run with verdicts, and the insight of the month - one thing known now that wasn't before.
4. **Present next month's plan as decisions** - Planned work tied to goals, plus any decisions needed from the client (approvals, budget moves) listed with deadlines.
5. **Keep the format frozen** - Same structure every month so trends are visible and preparation is fast. Format changes happen quarterly, announced, never mid-crisis.

## Deliverable

- Monthly report in the frozen format
- Decisions-required list with deadlines
- Updated rolling results vs baseline chart

## Success metrics

| Metric | How to read it |
|--------|---------------|
| Report read/engagement | Opened, discussed on the call, decisions taken from it |
| Decision turnaround | Days for client decisions listed in reports - tracked both ways |
| Renewal rate of reported accounts | The reporting system's ultimate score |

## Quality checklist

- [ ] Outcomes on page one, activity later
- [ ] Bad news present with explanation and response plan
- [ ] Same format as last month

## Guardrails

- Claims discipline: use only proof the user supplies or verifies. Never invent clients, results, rankings, or testimonials.
- Privacy and consent: collect and use personal data only with a lawful basis and consent where required. Respect PDPA (Singapore) and GDPR; honour unsubscribes and opt-outs immediately.
- Platform policy: follow each ad and search platform's current policies. When a rule changes often, flag it for a human to re-check before launch.
- Client data stays confidential and is used only for that client's work. Never reuse one client's data, results or creative for another client's pitch without written permission.
- Report client results honestly, including the misses. A client who discovers a hidden failure leaves; a client told early usually stays.
