# End-of-Month Performance Review (Client)

## What this skill does

Makes the monthly review a working session with outcomes - decisions made, priorities reset, trust maintained - instead of a screen-shared monologue.

## Inputs to collect

- Monthly reporting pack (sent as pre-read 48h ahead)
- Open decisions list from last month
- Next month's proposed plan and any budget asks

## Workflow

1. **Send the pre-read and the questions** - Report plus three named discussion questions 48 hours ahead. Meetings where the client first sees numbers live produce reactions, not decisions.
2. **Open with last month's decisions** - Each open decision: done, not done (why), or overtaken. This accountability loop is the meeting's spine.
3. **Discuss movements, not metrics** - Spend the time on the 2-3 biggest movements and their causes/plans. The full data is in the pre-read; the meeting is for judgement.
4. **Decide next month's priorities live** - Confirm the plan, the budget posture and the decisions the client owes (approvals, assets) with dates. Leave with a list both sides can recite.
5. **Close the loop in writing within 24h** - Decisions, owners and dates circulated the next day, and entered into the tracking system. Verbal agreements in monthly meetings evaporate on a predictable schedule.

## Deliverable

- Meeting decisions record (24h turnaround)
- Updated open-decisions list
- Confirmed next-month plan and client-side actions

## Success metrics

| Metric | How to read it |
|--------|---------------|
| Decision closure rate | Decisions from reviews completed by the next review |
| Pre-read engagement | Client arrives having read it - evidenced by the quality of questions |
| Meeting-to-plan adherence | Next month's delivery matches what the review agreed |

## Quality checklist

- [ ] Pre-read sent 48h ahead with named questions
- [ ] Last month's decisions reviewed first
- [ ] Written record circulated within 24 hours
