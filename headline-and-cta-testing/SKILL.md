# Headline and CTA Testing

## What this skill does

Builds a testing programme for the highest-leverage copy on the page, driven by customer language rather than copywriter roulette.

## Inputs to collect

- Current headline/CTA performance
- Voice-of-customer sources: reviews, calls, surveys
- Test design rules (from the A/B test design standard)

## Workflow

1. **Build the phrase bank** - Extract exact customer phrases for pains, outcomes and objections. Headline candidates come from this bank first, brainstorms second.
2. **Generate variants by angle** - Outcome-led, problem-led, mechanism-led, proof-led, question-led. One angle per variant so results teach you something reusable.
3. **Test headlines before buttons** - Headlines move comprehension; button text moves clarity of next step. Sequence tests: headline, then subhead/proof, then CTA wording - one at a time.
4. **Read results by segment** - Check new vs returning and source differences before declaring a winner. A headline that wins on warm traffic may lose on cold.
5. **Bank the learnings** - Winning angles and losing angles both go into the phrase bank with context. Next page starts from evidence, not from zero.

## Deliverable

- Voice-of-customer phrase bank
- Variant set with angle labels
- Results log with segment notes

## Success metrics

| Metric | How to read it |
|--------|---------------|
| Primary conversion lift per winning variant | With confidence recorded, not just percentages |
| Angle win-rate over time | Which promise types this audience believes |
| Test velocity on top pages | High-traffic pages should always be learning |

## Quality checklist

- [ ] Variants differ by angle, not by punctuation
- [ ] One variable per test
- [ ] Losing variants logged with the same care as winners

## Guardrails

- Claims discipline: use only proof the user supplies or verifies. Never invent clients, results, rankings, or testimonials.
- Privacy and consent: collect and use personal data only with a lawful basis and consent where required. Respect PDPA (Singapore) and GDPR; honour unsubscribes and opt-outs immediately.
- Platform policy: follow each ad and search platform's current policies. When a rule changes often, flag it for a human to re-check before launch.
- No dark patterns: no fake countdowns, confirm-shaming, hidden costs or pre-ticked add-ons. Conversion bought with deception is refunded with interest in trust.
- Social proof must be real: genuine testimonials with permission, accurate numbers, no fabricated reviews or logos.
