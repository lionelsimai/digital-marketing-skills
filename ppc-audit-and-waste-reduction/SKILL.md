# PPC Audit and Waste Reduction

## What this skill does

A structured, evidence-first audit that finds where money leaks and what to fix in what order, with savings estimated conservatively.

## Inputs to collect

- Account access or exports: campaigns, settings, search terms, conversions (90 days)
- Business targets: CPA/ROAS by line
- Change history for the period

## Workflow

1. **Verify the foundation** - Conversion tracking accuracy (test conversions, duplication, attribution window), enhanced conversions/consent state, and account access hygiene.
2. **Sweep the settings** - Geo presence, ad schedules, device/network settings (search partners, display network leakage), budget pacing and shared budgets hiding starved winners.
3. **Quantify term waste** - Spend with no conversions by theme and campaign; irrelevant-term spend as a percentage. Put a dollar figure on each leak.
4. **Review bidding and budgets** - Targets vs actuals, campaigns limited by budget while others waste, learning-period churn, and value quality (junk lead signals).
5. **Write the fix order** - Sequence by savings x speed: tracking fixes first, then settings, then terms, then structure. Each item: owner, effort, expected saving range.

## Deliverable

- Audit report with evidence and dollar-quantified leaks
- Fix list in priority order with owners
- 30-day waste-reduction tracker

## Success metrics

| Metric | How to read it |
|--------|---------------|
| Irrelevant spend percentage | Baseline vs 30 days post-fix |
| Tracking discrepancy rate | Platform conversions vs CRM/backend truth |
| Recovered budget redeployed | Savings moved to proven campaigns, not pocketed silently |

## Quality checklist

- [ ] Savings estimates labelled conservative and evidence-based
- [ ] No structural rebuild recommended where settings fixes suffice
- [ ] Audit distinguishes errors from deliberate past decisions

## Guardrails

- Claims discipline: use only proof the user supplies or verifies. Never invent clients, results, rankings, or testimonials.
- Privacy and consent: collect and use personal data only with a lawful basis and consent where required. Respect PDPA (Singapore) and GDPR; honour unsubscribes and opt-outs immediately.
- Platform policy: follow each ad and search platform's current policies. When a rule changes often, flag it for a human to re-check before launch.
- Ad policy compliance: no misleading claims, no prohibited content, no trademark abuse. Ads must promise only what the landing page delivers.
- Never advertise results, prices or offers the business cannot honour today.
