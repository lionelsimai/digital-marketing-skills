# Marketing Budget Allocation

## What this skill does

Turns a lump sum into a defensible allocation: how much to proven channels, how much to tests, and what each dollar is expected to return.

## Inputs to collect

- Total budget and period
- Last period's cost per lead/sale by channel
- Margin per sale and target growth rate

## Workflow

1. **Start from unit economics** - Compute allowable acquisition cost from margin, close rate and target payback. Channels that cannot hit it are cut or fixed first, not funded.
2. **Split by job, then channel** - Allocate to demand capture, demand creation and retention first (working split: 50/30/20 for most B2B), then assign channels inside each job.
3. **Ring-fence a test budget** - Hold 10-15% for structured tests with pre-set kill criteria. Tests without kill criteria are just spend.
4. **Check capacity constraints** - Cap channels at what the team can actually service: sales follow-up capacity, content production, creative output.
5. **Set reallocation rules** - Write the monthly rule in advance: what performance moves budget, by how much, and who approves. Decide before results create arguments.

## Deliverable

- Budget table by job, channel and month
- Allowable CAC per segment with the maths
- Reallocation rules and test kill criteria

## Success metrics

| Metric | How to read it |
|--------|---------------|
| Blended CAC vs allowable CAC | The single control number for the whole budget |
| Test budget hit rate | Share of tests that earn scale-up; aim 1 in 4 |
| Budget utilisation vs pacing | Underspend on capture channels usually means a targeting or offer problem |

## Quality checklist

- [ ] No channel funded above its proven capacity to convert
- [ ] Test budget separated from scale budget
- [ ] Reallocation rules written before the period starts

## Guardrails

- Claims discipline: use only proof the user supplies or verifies. Never invent clients, results, rankings, or testimonials.
- Privacy and consent: collect and use personal data only with a lawful basis and consent where required. Respect PDPA (Singapore) and GDPR; honour unsubscribes and opt-outs immediately.
- Platform policy: follow each ad and search platform's current policies. When a rule changes often, flag it for a human to re-check before launch.
- Strategy before channels: never recommend a channel or budget split without the offer, audience and unit economics in hand.
