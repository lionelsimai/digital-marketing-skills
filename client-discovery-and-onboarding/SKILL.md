# Client Discovery and Onboarding

## What this skill does

Front-loads everything engagements usually die from later: unclear goals, missing access, unknown stakeholders and unspoken expectations.

## Inputs to collect

- Signed scope and commercial terms
- Client's business model, goals and past marketing history
- Access list: analytics, ad accounts, site, CRM, brand assets

## Workflow

1. **Run the discovery session** - 90 minutes on: how money is made, who the customer is, what worked/failed before, who decides, and what success looks like at day 90 in their words.
2. **Collect access methodically** - Access checklist with the client's IT/owner involved from day one; logins via their password manager or delegated access - never shared passwords over email.
3. **Baseline the numbers** - Current funnel, channel performance and data quality, documented and agreed. The baseline is the contract every later report is judged against.
4. **Map the stakeholders** - Decision-maker, day-to-day contact, approvers, blockers. Communication rules per person (who gets reports, who joins calls, response-time expectations on both sides).
5. **Publish the first-30-days plan** - Week-by-week: audit, quick fixes, foundations, first campaigns - with what the client must provide by when. Shared in writing within the first week.

## Deliverable

- Discovery notes and agreed goals document
- Access checklist with completion status
- Baseline report and first-30-days plan

## Success metrics

| Metric | How to read it |
|--------|---------------|
| Time to full access | Days from signing to all systems live - track and shrink it |
| Baseline agreement rate | Baselines signed off by the client before work is judged |
| Day-30 expectation match | Client survey: did the first month match the plan? |

## Quality checklist

- [ ] Goals restated in the client's words and confirmed
- [ ] No work starts on accounts the agency can't properly access
- [ ] Stakeholder map includes the person who can say no

## Guardrails

- Claims discipline: use only proof the user supplies or verifies. Never invent clients, results, rankings, or testimonials.
- Privacy and consent: collect and use personal data only with a lawful basis and consent where required. Respect PDPA (Singapore) and GDPR; honour unsubscribes and opt-outs immediately.
- Platform policy: follow each ad and search platform's current policies. When a rule changes often, flag it for a human to re-check before launch.
- Client data stays confidential and is used only for that client's work. Never reuse one client's data, results or creative for another client's pitch without written permission.
- Report client results honestly, including the misses. A client who discovers a hidden failure leaves; a client told early usually stays.
