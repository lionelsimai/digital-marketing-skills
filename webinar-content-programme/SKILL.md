# Webinar Content Programme

## What this skill does

Builds webinars that teach genuinely and convert honestly - with the promotion and follow-up systems most teams skip.

## Inputs to collect

- Audience and their current burning questions
- Speaker(s) with real expertise and availability
- Platform, registration and email tooling

## Workflow

1. **Pick a teaching topic** - A topic the audience would pay to learn, that naturally demonstrates the offer's mechanism. Product demos disguised as education empty rooms.
2. **Design the session** - 45-60 min: hook with the problem, teach the framework, live example or teardown, Q&A, then a clear, proportionate next-step offer at the end.
3. **Promote with a runway** - 2-3 weeks: email the list, partner co-promotion, speaker's channels, reminder sequence (registration, day-before, hour-before). Track source per registration.
4. **Run the follow-up split** - Attendees, no-shows and high-engagement segments get different follow-ups within 24 hours: replay + resource for all, conversation offer for the engaged.
5. **Repurpose systematically** - Recording to clips, Q&A to FAQ content, slides to a lead magnet. One webinar should yield a month of assets.

## Deliverable

- Session plan and slide outline
- Promotion plan with source tracking
- Segmented follow-up sequences and repurposing list

## Success metrics

| Metric | How to read it |
|--------|---------------|
| Registration-to-attendance rate | 30-40% is typical for free B2B; reminders move it |
| Engaged attendee rate | Stayed 30+ min or asked/answered - the real audience |
| Pipeline from attendees within 60 days | The programme's business case |

## Quality checklist

- [ ] Teaching content stands alone without the pitch
- [ ] Follow-up segments and emails written before the event
- [ ] Recording consent and privacy notices handled

## Guardrails

- Claims discipline: use only proof the user supplies or verifies. Never invent clients, results, rankings, or testimonials.
- Privacy and consent: collect and use personal data only with a lawful basis and consent where required. Respect PDPA (Singapore) and GDPR; honour unsubscribes and opt-outs immediately.
- Platform policy: follow each ad and search platform's current policies. When a rule changes often, flag it for a human to re-check before launch.
- Original or properly licensed content only. No plagiarism, no scraped articles, no AI-spun rewrites of competitors' work.
- Statistics and quotes must trace to a real source the reader can check.
