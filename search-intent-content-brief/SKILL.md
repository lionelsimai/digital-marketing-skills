# Search Intent Content Brief

## What this skill does

Prevents the most common content failure - a well-written article that answers the wrong question - by deciding intent, scope and evidence up front.

## Inputs to collect

- Target cluster and SERP notes
- Business angle: what this brand can say that others cannot
- Available proof, data and expert sources

## Workflow

1. **State the intent in one line** - Write what the searcher wants to accomplish and what a satisfying answer looks like. Attach the SERP format evidence.
2. **Define the angle and promise** - The specific claim this piece will make and the reader outcome it delivers. If there is no angle, the topic goes back to research.
3. **Outline the structure** - H2/H3 skeleton in answer order, with the key fact, example or data point each section must include.
4. **Specify proof and links** - List required sources, internal links (target pages and anchors) and the CTA that fits the intent stage.
5. **Set acceptance criteria** - Word range as guidance (not a target), originality requirements, and what the editor checks before publish.

## Deliverable

- One-page brief: intent, angle, outline, proof, links, CTA
- Source list with required citations
- Editor acceptance checklist

## Success metrics

| Metric | How to read it |
|--------|---------------|
| Brief-to-publish cycle time | Good briefs shorten rewrites |
| Ranking outcome per brief | Share of briefed pieces reaching top 10 for their cluster |
| Rewrite rate | Falling rewrite requests signal brief quality |

## Quality checklist

- [ ] Intent statement matches the live SERP
- [ ] Angle is something competitors have not already published verbatim
- [ ] No brief asks a writer to invent statistics or quotes

## Guardrails

- Claims discipline: use only proof the user supplies or verifies. Never invent clients, results, rankings, or testimonials.
- Privacy and consent: collect and use personal data only with a lawful basis and consent where required. Respect PDPA (Singapore) and GDPR; honour unsubscribes and opt-outs immediately.
- Platform policy: follow each ad and search platform's current policies. When a rule changes often, flag it for a human to re-check before launch.
- White-hat only: no purchased links, link schemes, PBNs, keyword stuffing, cloaking or scraped/spun content. Earn coverage and links with assets worth citing.
