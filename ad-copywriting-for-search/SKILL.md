# Ad Copywriting for Search

## What this skill does

Produces ad copy built from the searcher's query language and the business's verified proof - not generic superlatives.

## Inputs to collect

- Keyword themes and query examples
- Landing page content and verified proof points
- Brand voice rules and any compliance constraints

## Workflow

1. **Mirror the query** - Headline 1 restates what the searcher asked for in their terms. If the ad could run for any query, it matches none.
2. **Assign each headline one job** - Promise, proof, differentiator, offer, CTA - each headline does one. Fifteen variations of the same claim waste the asset.
3. **Pin sparingly** - Pin only the promise and legal/compliance lines. Let the system test the rest; review combinations, not just individual headlines.
4. **Write descriptions as closers** - Descriptions expand the promise with specifics: what's included, how it works, what happens next. No claims the page doesn't support.
5. **Plan the refresh cycle** - Set a monthly review: retire bottom assets, write challengers against the top performer, log learnings by theme.

## Deliverable

- RSA sets per ad group: headlines, descriptions, pinning plan
- Copy-to-proof mapping (each claim and its source)
- Monthly refresh log and challenger plan

## Success metrics

| Metric | How to read it |
|--------|---------------|
| CTR by ad group | Against the same group's history, not account averages |
| Ad strength + combination reports | Use as diagnostics, not as the goal |
| Conversion rate of ad traffic | Clicks that don't convert mean the page or promise is wrong |

## Quality checklist

- [ ] Every claim in copy exists on the landing page
- [ ] No competitor trademarks used improperly in copy
- [ ] Restricted claims (health, finance, housing) checked against platform rules

## Guardrails

- Claims discipline: use only proof the user supplies or verifies. Never invent clients, results, rankings, or testimonials.
- Privacy and consent: collect and use personal data only with a lawful basis and consent where required. Respect PDPA (Singapore) and GDPR; honour unsubscribes and opt-outs immediately.
- Platform policy: follow each ad and search platform's current policies. When a rule changes often, flag it for a human to re-check before launch.
- Ad policy compliance: no misleading claims, no prohibited content, no trademark abuse. Ads must promise only what the landing page delivers.
- Never advertise results, prices or offers the business cannot honour today.
