# Winback and Reactivation

## What this skill does

Recovers the recoverable and releases the rest - a winback that respects the relationship and improves list health either way.

## Inputs to collect

- Lapse definition by stage (days since purchase/engagement)
- What has changed since they left: product, content, pricing
- Incentive policy and margin limits

## Workflow

1. **Define lapse honestly** - Set lapsing windows from actual purchase/engagement cycles, not impatience. B2B annual buyers are not lapsed at 90 days.
2. **Lead with what's new** - Email 1: the strongest genuine change or best content since they left. 'We miss you' with nothing new is noise.
3. **Ask and listen** - Email 2: preference reset or one-question survey (too many emails? wrong topics? price?). Route answers into segments; a reply is a win even without a purchase.
4. **Make the final offer or goodbye** - Email 3: the best honest offer if policy allows, or a graceful last chance with a one-click stay. Then honour the sunset - move non-responders out of regular sends.
5. **Measure both outcomes** - Reactivated revenue AND list health gain from sunsetting the rest. Both are returns on the campaign.

## Deliverable

- Winback series (3 emails) with segment routing
- Sunset list and suppression handling
- Reactivation + list-health results report

## Success metrics

| Metric | How to read it |
|--------|---------------|
| Reactivation rate by lapse band | Recent lapses should recover at multiples of old ones |
| Revenue per winback send | Against the cost of the incentive, if any |
| Post-sunset deliverability trend | The hidden dividend |

## Quality checklist

- [ ] Sunset actually executed after the series
- [ ] Incentives (if any) within margin policy and honestly framed
- [ ] Survey responses routed to segments, not a dead inbox

## Guardrails

- Claims discipline: use only proof the user supplies or verifies. Never invent clients, results, rankings, or testimonials.
- Privacy and consent: collect and use personal data only with a lawful basis and consent where required. Respect PDPA (Singapore) and GDPR; honour unsubscribes and opt-outs immediately.
- Platform policy: follow each ad and search platform's current policies. When a rule changes often, flag it for a human to re-check before launch.
- Permission-based sending only: no purchased or scraped lists, ever. Clear consent, honest sender identity, one-click unsubscribe in every email.
- Deliverability is trust: no deceptive subject lines, no hidden downsides, no dark patterns in preference or unsubscribe flows.
