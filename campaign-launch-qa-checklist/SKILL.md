# Campaign Launch QA Checklist

## What this skill does

The checklist that stands between a campaign and its most expensive first day: every launch verified by a second person against a written standard.

## Inputs to collect

- Campaign build across platforms
- Landing pages, forms and tracking setup
- Approval records (client/stakeholder sign-offs)

## Workflow

1. **Verify tracking end to end** - Click a live-preview ad, complete the conversion, and find the event in analytics and CRM with correct values and UTMs. If you can't find it, neither can the optimisation algorithms.
2. **Check every destination** - All URLs live, correct, fast on mobile, with the right page per ad group/cell - no 404s, no homepage dumping for specific ads.
3. **Audit the settings** - Budgets (daily vs lifetime traps), dates and time zones, geo and audience inclusions/exclusions, bid strategies and caps, frequency settings.
4. **Proof the creative and copy** - Spelling, claims against the claims register, legal lines present, brand assets current, accessibility basics on landing pages.
5. **Confirm approvals and rollback** - Written approval on file for budget and creative; the pause/rollback steps and who can execute them outside office hours are written on the checklist itself.

## Deliverable

- Signed launch QA checklist per campaign
- Tracking verification evidence (screenshots/IDs)
- Rollback plan with named executors

## Success metrics

| Metric | How to read it |
|--------|---------------|
| Launch defects found in QA | The checklist earning its existence |
| Day-1 incident rate | Campaigns needing emergency fixes after launch - target near zero |
| Tracking verification pass rate | Campaigns whose conversion was found end-to-end pre-launch |

## Quality checklist

- [ ] QA performed by someone other than the builder
- [ ] Test conversion found in every downstream system
- [ ] Rollback plan exists and names real people

## Guardrails

- Claims discipline: use only proof the user supplies or verifies. Never invent clients, results, rankings, or testimonials.
- Privacy and consent: collect and use personal data only with a lawful basis and consent where required. Respect PDPA (Singapore) and GDPR; honour unsubscribes and opt-outs immediately.
- Platform policy: follow each ad and search platform's current policies. When a rule changes often, flag it for a human to re-check before launch.
- Client data stays confidential and is used only for that client's work. Never reuse one client's data, results or creative for another client's pitch without written permission.
- Report client results honestly, including the misses. A client who discovers a hidden failure leaves; a client told early usually stays.
