# Content Strategy Framework

## What this skill does

Defines what content exists to do, for whom, and how success is judged - so production stops being a random act of publishing.

## Inputs to collect

- Business goals and priority audiences
- Funnel map and keyword map (if they exist)
- Current content inventory and performance

## Workflow

1. **Assign content its jobs** - Per funnel stage: attract (search/social discovery), convince (comparison, proof), convert (offer content), retain (customer education). Name the primary job per quarter.
2. **Pick the pillars** - 3-5 topic pillars where the brand has real expertise and the audience has real demand. Pillars outside your expertise are where content goes to be mediocre.
3. **Set the formats** - Match formats to jobs and production reality: articles, tools, data studies, video, templates. A format you can't sustain is a plan to quit.
4. **Define success per job** - Attract = qualified organic sessions; convince = assisted conversions; convert = direct conversions; retain = customer engagement/expansion. Write them down.
5. **Write the say-no list** - Topics and formats you will not chase this year (trend-jacking outside pillars, content with no distribution path). The no list protects the yes list.

## Deliverable

- Content strategy: jobs, pillars, formats, success metrics
- Pillar-to-funnel map
- Say-no list for the year

## Success metrics

| Metric | How to read it |
|--------|---------------|
| Content-assisted pipeline/revenue | By pillar, quarterly |
| Share of content with a distribution plan | Publish-and-pray content trends to zero |
| Production consistency | Planned vs shipped per month |

## Quality checklist

- [ ] Every pillar is an area of genuine expertise
- [ ] Success defined per funnel job, not as 'traffic'
- [ ] Capacity matches the publishing promise

## Guardrails

- Claims discipline: use only proof the user supplies or verifies. Never invent clients, results, rankings, or testimonials.
- Privacy and consent: collect and use personal data only with a lawful basis and consent where required. Respect PDPA (Singapore) and GDPR; honour unsubscribes and opt-outs immediately.
- Platform policy: follow each ad and search platform's current policies. When a rule changes often, flag it for a human to re-check before launch.
- Original or properly licensed content only. No plagiarism, no scraped articles, no AI-spun rewrites of competitors' work.
- Statistics and quotes must trace to a real source the reader can check.
