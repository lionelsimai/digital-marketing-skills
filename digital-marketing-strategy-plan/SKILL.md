# Digital Marketing Strategy Plan

## What this skill does

Turns business goals into one coherent strategy document an owner or team can execute for a quarter, instead of a pile of disconnected channel tactics.

## Inputs to collect

- Business model and offer, with prices and gross margin
- Revenue or pipeline goal for the next 12 months
- Current channels, spend and results for the last 2 quarters

## Workflow

1. **Set the commercial goal** - Translate the business goal into marketing targets: revenue, qualified leads or trials, working back from price, close rate and margin to the traffic and conversion volumes required.
2. **Audit the current state** - Review funnel numbers stage by stage, channel performance, and the website's conversion rate. Name the single biggest leak before planning anything new.
3. **Define audiences and offer angles** - Pick 1-3 priority segments and the specific promise each one hears. One audience per plan section; no 'everyone' targeting.
4. **Assign channel roles** - Give each channel one job: capture demand (search), create demand (paid social, content), or convert and retain (email, CRO). Set a budget share per role, not per platform whim.
5. **Build the 90-day roadmap** - Sequence work into monthly themes with owners and dates: fix the leak first, then scale capture, then add demand creation.

## Deliverable

- Strategy document: goals, audiences, channel roles, budget split
- Funnel targets per stage with the maths shown
- 90-day roadmap with owners and monthly themes

## Success metrics

| Metric | How to read it |
|--------|---------------|
| Cost per qualified outcome | Blended across channels; the strategy fails if this rises while volume targets are hit |
| Funnel conversion rate by stage | Compare against the audit baseline each month |
| Revenue or pipeline vs plan | Reviewed monthly, not at quarter end |

## Quality checklist

- [ ] Every tactic traces to a goal and a funnel stage
- [ ] Budget maths reconciles with margin and close rate
- [ ] One priority leak named, not a list of ten

## Guardrails

- Claims discipline: use only proof the user supplies or verifies. Never invent clients, results, rankings, or testimonials.
- Privacy and consent: collect and use personal data only with a lawful basis and consent where required. Respect PDPA (Singapore) and GDPR; honour unsubscribes and opt-outs immediately.
- Platform policy: follow each ad and search platform's current policies. When a rule changes often, flag it for a human to re-check before launch.
- Strategy before channels: never recommend a channel or budget split without the offer, audience and unit economics in hand.
