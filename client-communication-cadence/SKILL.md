# Client Communication Cadence

## What this skill does

Designs communication as a system with tempos and purposes, so clients feel informed without being spammed - and problems surface early by design.

## Inputs to collect

- Stakeholder map from onboarding
- Engagement scope and reporting commitments
- Client preferences (tools, meeting tolerance, time zones)

## Workflow

1. **Set the three tempos** - Weekly async update (shipped, in progress, blocked, needs-you), monthly performance review (the report call), quarterly strategy session (goals, roadmap, relationship). Each with a fixed day and owner.
2. **Write the weekly update standard** - Five lines maximum by default: done, doing, stuck, decision needed, metric snapshot. If it needs a scroll, it needs a call instead.
3. **Define the escalation path** - What counts as urgent (site down, spend anomaly, PR issue), the channel for it, and response-time commitments both directions. Test it once with a drill message.
4. **Install the no-surprises rule** - Bad news travels within one business day of discovery, with a plan attached. Both sides agree the rule explicitly in onboarding - it's a trust feature, not a slogan.
5. **Review the cadence quarterly** - Ask the client: too much, too little, wrong format? Adjust deliberately and note the change. Communication preferences are data, not defaults.

## Deliverable

- Cadence charter: tempos, owners, channels, SLAs
- Weekly update template
- Escalation path card shared with the client

## Success metrics

| Metric | How to read it |
|--------|---------------|
| Weekly update consistency | Sent on schedule - the trust metronome |
| Surprise incidents | Times the client learned material news late or from someone else - target zero |
| Client responsiveness to decisions | Cadence quality shows up as faster client replies too |

## Quality checklist

- [ ] Escalation path agreed and tested
- [ ] No-surprises rule stated to the client explicitly
- [ ] Every tempo has a named owner on the agency side

## Guardrails

- Claims discipline: use only proof the user supplies or verifies. Never invent clients, results, rankings, or testimonials.
- Privacy and consent: collect and use personal data only with a lawful basis and consent where required. Respect PDPA (Singapore) and GDPR; honour unsubscribes and opt-outs immediately.
- Platform policy: follow each ad and search platform's current policies. When a rule changes often, flag it for a human to re-check before launch.
- Client data stays confidential and is used only for that client's work. Never reuse one client's data, results or creative for another client's pitch without written permission.
- Report client results honestly, including the misses. A client who discovers a hidden failure leaves; a client told early usually stays.
