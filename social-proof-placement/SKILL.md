# Social Proof Placement

## What this skill does

Turns a proof library into a placement system: the right evidence, from the right kind of customer, at the exact scroll-depth where doubt peaks.

## Inputs to collect

- Proof library: testimonials (with permissions), results data, client logos (approved), credentials
- Page list with each page's decision point
- Audience segments and their specific doubts

## Workflow

1. **Inventory and verify the proof** - Log every proof item: source, permission status, date, the claim it supports. Anything unverifiable or unpermissioned is excluded - no exceptions.
2. **Match proof to doubt** - Price doubt needs value/results proof; trust doubt needs credentials and longevity; fit doubt needs 'people like me' testimonials. Map per page section.
3. **Prefer specifics over volume** - One detailed testimonial with a name, role and result beats twenty anonymous five-star snippets. Specificity is what makes proof believable.
4. **Place at the moment of ask** - Proof sits beside the claim and before the CTA it supports - not in a footer graveyard. On forms, a short reassurance line near the submit button.
5. **Keep it current** - Refresh dates and results quarterly; retire stale proof. Old numbers presented as current are a lie of omission.

## Deliverable

- Verified proof inventory with permissions
- Per-page proof placement map
- Proof refresh calendar

## Success metrics

| Metric | How to read it |
|--------|---------------|
| Conversion rate on pages with placed proof vs before | Measured per page, honestly controlled where possible |
| Proof coverage of key claims | Share of major claims with adjacent evidence |
| Stale-proof count | Items past their review date - target zero |

## Quality checklist

- [ ] Permission on file for every name, logo and quote used
- [ ] Numbers match their source records exactly
- [ ] No stock-photo 'customers' or invented review counts

## Guardrails

- Claims discipline: use only proof the user supplies or verifies. Never invent clients, results, rankings, or testimonials.
- Privacy and consent: collect and use personal data only with a lawful basis and consent where required. Respect PDPA (Singapore) and GDPR; honour unsubscribes and opt-outs immediately.
- Platform policy: follow each ad and search platform's current policies. When a rule changes often, flag it for a human to re-check before launch.
- No dark patterns: no fake countdowns, confirm-shaming, hidden costs or pre-ticked add-ons. Conversion bought with deception is refunded with interest in trust.
- Social proof must be real: genuine testimonials with permission, accurate numbers, no fabricated reviews or logos.
- Fake reviews, purchased testimonials and unapproved client logos are prohibited - legally and reputationally radioactive.
