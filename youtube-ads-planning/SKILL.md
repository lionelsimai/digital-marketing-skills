# YouTube Ads Planning

## What this skill does

Turns YouTube from 'upload the TV cut' into a planned channel: format matched to job, creative built for skippable reality, and honest measurement.

## Inputs to collect

- Video assets available or production capacity
- Audience signals: search behaviour, site visitors, customer lists
- Funnel job for video (demand creation vs capture support)

## Workflow

1. **Match format to job** - Skippable in-stream for efficient reach with intent, in-feed for discovery, Shorts for volume and testing hooks, bumpers for reminder frequency.
2. **Build audiences from intent** - Custom intent/search-term audiences, channel/content placements for niche topics, and remarketing layers for viewers and site visitors.
3. **Brief creative for the skip** - Brand and hook inside 5 seconds, one message per video, end card with a next step. Produce a hook matrix: same body, 3-4 different openings.
4. **Set frequency and sequencing** - Cap frequency per week; where the story needs it, sequence (problem video then proof video) with audience rules.
5. **Measure beyond views** - Track view rate, watch time, earned actions, branded search lift and assisted conversions. A view is exposure, not success.

## Deliverable

- YouTube plan: formats, audiences, flights
- Creative hook matrix and production brief
- Measurement sheet: view, lift and assist metrics

## Success metrics

| Metric | How to read it |
|--------|---------------|
| View rate by hook variant | The hook test is the fastest creative learning |
| Branded search lift in exposed geos/periods | Video's most honest demand signal |
| Cost per engaged view / per assisted conversion | Keeps optimisation honest |

## Quality checklist

- [ ] Hook and brand land inside 5 seconds
- [ ] Claims in video match landing page and policy rules
- [ ] Frequency caps set before launch

## Guardrails

- Claims discipline: use only proof the user supplies or verifies. Never invent clients, results, rankings, or testimonials.
- Privacy and consent: collect and use personal data only with a lawful basis and consent where required. Respect PDPA (Singapore) and GDPR; honour unsubscribes and opt-outs immediately.
- Platform policy: follow each ad and search platform's current policies. When a rule changes often, flag it for a human to re-check before launch.
- Buy media the business can measure. If a placement cannot be tracked or brand-lift measured, size it as an experiment, not a pillar.
