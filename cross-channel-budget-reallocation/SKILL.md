# Cross-Channel Budget Reallocation

## What this skill does

Makes budget movement a disciplined, evidence-based routine instead of weekly whiplash driven by whichever dashboard someone opened.

## Inputs to collect

- Channel performance with consistent attribution window
- Marginal cost data (performance at recent spend levels)
- Business constraints: minimum presence, contracts, capacity

## Workflow

1. **Compare marginal, not average** - Rank channels by the cost of the NEXT conversion at current spend. Averages reward channels that were good at half the budget.
2. **Set movement rules** - Move in bounded steps (e.g. 10-20% of a channel's budget per cycle), with a cooling-off period to read results before moving again.
3. **Protect strategic minimums** - Ring-fence brand defence and any channel whose value is delayed (content, video) with separate evaluation windows - don't let weekly CPA kill quarterly assets.
4. **Execute and log** - Every move logged: from, to, amount, reason, expected effect, review date. The log is the audit trail when results are debated.
5. **Review the move, not the mood** - At the review date, compare against the logged expectation and decide: hold, extend, or reverse. Reversals are data, not failure.

## Deliverable

- Marginal-return ranking table
- Reallocation rules and minimums document
- Budget movement log with review dates

## Success metrics

| Metric | How to read it |
|--------|---------------|
| Blended CAC trend across moves | The reallocation programme's scorecard |
| Move success rate | Share of moves that improved blended results at review |
| Budget concentration risk | No single channel carrying an unsafe share of outcomes |

## Quality checklist

- [ ] Rules written before performance is reviewed
- [ ] Delayed-value channels evaluated on their own window
- [ ] Every move has a logged reason and review date

## Guardrails

- Claims discipline: use only proof the user supplies or verifies. Never invent clients, results, rankings, or testimonials.
- Privacy and consent: collect and use personal data only with a lawful basis and consent where required. Respect PDPA (Singapore) and GDPR; honour unsubscribes and opt-outs immediately.
- Platform policy: follow each ad and search platform's current policies. When a rule changes often, flag it for a human to re-check before launch.
- Buy media the business can measure. If a placement cannot be tracked or brand-lift measured, size it as an experiment, not a pillar.
