# Audience Persona Research

## What this skill does

Produces 2-4 personas grounded in what real customers say and do, each with the trigger event, pains, objections and language an advertiser or copywriter can use the same day.

## Inputs to collect

- Customer list or CRM export (anonymised is fine)
- Access to reviews, support tickets or sales call notes
- Any existing persona or segmentation documents

## Workflow

1. **Mine the evidence** - Read 30-50 raw sources: reviews, tickets, call notes, survey verbatims. Extract exact phrases for pains, desired outcomes and objections.
2. **Find the trigger events** - Group buyers by what happened just before they bought (new role, audit, growth pain, incident). Trigger beats demographics for targeting and copy.
3. **Draft the personas** - One page each: segment, trigger, top 3 pains in their words, top 3 objections, decision process, where they spend attention.
4. **Validate against the data** - Check each persona maps to a real share of customers or revenue. Merge or drop personas that exist only in someone's imagination.
5. **Write the use rules** - State what each persona changes: ad angle, landing page headline, email sequence, sales talk track. A persona that changes nothing gets deleted.

## Deliverable

- 2-4 validated one-page personas with verbatim language
- Trigger-event list ranked by revenue share
- Persona-to-message map for ads, pages and email

## Success metrics

| Metric | How to read it |
|--------|---------------|
| Share of revenue covered | Personas should cover 80%+ of real customers |
| Message match test | Landing conversion when copy uses persona language vs generic copy |
| Persona usage | Count of campaigns and pages actually built from the personas |

## Quality checklist

- [ ] Every claim in a persona traces to a source quote or data point
- [ ] No persona built from demographics alone
- [ ] Objections include price and trust, not just features

## Guardrails

- Claims discipline: use only proof the user supplies or verifies. Never invent clients, results, rankings, or testimonials.
- Privacy and consent: collect and use personal data only with a lawful basis and consent where required. Respect PDPA (Singapore) and GDPR; honour unsubscribes and opt-outs immediately.
- Platform policy: follow each ad and search platform's current policies. When a rule changes often, flag it for a human to re-check before launch.
- Strategy before channels: never recommend a channel or budget split without the offer, audience and unit economics in hand.
