# Channel Mix Planning

## What this skill does

Evaluates candidate channels against audience presence, intent, cost and operational load, and produces a sequenced mix instead of a presence on every platform.

## Inputs to collect

- Target audience and where they already spend attention
- Offer price point and sales cycle length
- Team size and content/creative production capacity

## Workflow

1. **Score candidate channels** - Rate each on audience fit, buying intent, cost to test, time to first signal and team load. Score honestly: a cheap channel the team cannot feed is expensive.
2. **Match channel to funnel job** - Search captures existing intent; paid social and content create it; email and community monetise it. A mix missing a job will leak.
3. **Sequence the rollout** - Start with 2-3 channels maximum: the highest-intent capture channel, one demand channel, and owned capture (email list). Add a channel only when the existing ones are stable.
4. **Define exit and scale criteria** - Per channel: the test budget, the signal that earns more budget, and the signal that ends the test. Written before launch.
5. **Plan the owned-asset backbone** - Every rented channel must point at an owned asset (list, site, community). Note what each channel feeds.

## Deliverable

- Channel scorecard with sequenced picks
- Per-channel test budget and kill/scale criteria
- Owned-asset map showing what each channel feeds

## Success metrics

| Metric | How to read it |
|--------|---------------|
| Time to first qualified signal per channel | Days and dollars to the first real lead or sale |
| Channel CAC trend after 90 days | Early CAC lies; judge on the second quarter |
| Owned audience growth rate | The mix is working if rented reach converts into owned reach |

## Quality checklist

- [ ] No more than 3 channels in the starting mix
- [ ] Each channel has a written kill criterion
- [ ] At least one owned destination exists before spend starts

## Guardrails

- Claims discipline: use only proof the user supplies or verifies. Never invent clients, results, rankings, or testimonials.
- Privacy and consent: collect and use personal data only with a lawful basis and consent where required. Respect PDPA (Singapore) and GDPR; honour unsubscribes and opt-outs immediately.
- Platform policy: follow each ad and search platform's current policies. When a rule changes often, flag it for a human to re-check before launch.
- Strategy before channels: never recommend a channel or budget split without the offer, audience and unit economics in hand.
