# Pricing Page Optimization

## What this skill does

Makes the pricing page do its real job - help the right buyer choose the right plan confidently - instead of hiding the price behind a sales call out of habit.

## Inputs to collect

- Plans, prices and the differences that actually matter
- Sales data: which plans sell, where deals stall on price
- Competitor pricing presentation (public)

## Workflow

1. **Simplify the architecture** - 3 plans is a strong default. Differentiate on dimensions buyers understand (users, volume, support level), not feature soup. Name the audience each plan is for.
2. **Anchor value before price** - Each plan leads with the outcome and who it's for; the price follows. Show the cost of the problem or the unit economics where you honestly can.
3. **Make comparison scannable** - Feature table with the differences that drive choice at the top; identical rows collapsed. Highlight the recommended plan for the core segment - because it's recommended, not because it's priciest.
4. **Handle the price objections inline** - Billing toggle clarity (monthly/annual with the real saving), what's included, taxes/fees honesty, cancellation terms, and a real FAQ from sales calls.
5. **Add risk reversal at the decision** - Trial, pilot, money-back or onboarding guarantee - only what operations honours happily. Place it at the CTA, not buried in terms.

## Deliverable

- Redesigned pricing page spec: architecture, copy, table, FAQ
- Objection log from sales with page answers
- Test plan for the biggest uncertainty (layout vs framing vs anchor)

## Success metrics

| Metric | How to read it |
|--------|---------------|
| Pricing-to-signup/purchase conversion | By plan mix, not just total |
| Recommended-plan share | Choice architecture working as designed |
| Price-related support/sales questions per 100 visits | Comprehension measured in reduced confusion |

## Quality checklist

- [ ] Total real cost visible without tricks or drip pricing
- [ ] Recommended plan justified by fit, labelled honestly
- [ ] Every guarantee on the page is operationally honoured

## Guardrails

- Claims discipline: use only proof the user supplies or verifies. Never invent clients, results, rankings, or testimonials.
- Privacy and consent: collect and use personal data only with a lawful basis and consent where required. Respect PDPA (Singapore) and GDPR; honour unsubscribes and opt-outs immediately.
- Platform policy: follow each ad and search platform's current policies. When a rule changes often, flag it for a human to re-check before launch.
- No dark patterns: no fake countdowns, confirm-shaming, hidden costs or pre-ticked add-ons. Conversion bought with deception is refunded with interest in trust.
- Social proof must be real: genuine testimonials with permission, accurate numbers, no fabricated reviews or logos.
- No decoy pricing games, fake 'was' prices, or savings claims that need an asterisk to be true.
