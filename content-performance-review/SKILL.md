# Content Performance Review

## What this skill does

A decision meeting disguised as a report: every piece of content gets a verdict - scale, refresh, consolidate or retire - with evidence.

## Inputs to collect

- Content inventory with traffic, conversion and distribution data
- Keyword map and cluster assignments
- Production cost/time per piece (estimates acceptable)

## Workflow

1. **Score the library** - Per piece: qualified traffic, conversions/assists, backlinks, production cost. Four quadrants emerge: stars, hidden gems, zombies, dogs.
2. **Interrogate the stars** - What do winners share - topic, format, intent, distribution? Write the pattern down; it's the next quarter's brief filter.
3. **Decide the zombies** - Traffic but no outcome: fix the CTA/intent fit, consolidate into a stronger page, or accept as brand content with eyes open.
4. **Retire or redirect the dogs** - No traffic, no strategic role: merge, redirect or delete with a redirect map. Dead weight dilutes the site's focus.
5. **Set next quarter's shortlist** - Production list derived from winner patterns and cluster gaps - not from whoever shouted loudest.

## Deliverable

- Library scorecard with verdict per piece
- Refresh/consolidate/retire action list
- Next-quarter production shortlist with rationale

## Success metrics

| Metric | How to read it |
|--------|---------------|
| Share of library earning its keep | Pieces meeting their job's success metric |
| Assists per production dollar/hour | The content P&L in one ratio |
| Verdict completion rate | Actions from last review actually done |

## Quality checklist

- [ ] Every piece gets a verdict, none are skipped
- [ ] Retirements include a redirect plan
- [ ] Winner patterns stated as testable rules, not vibes

## Guardrails

- Claims discipline: use only proof the user supplies or verifies. Never invent clients, results, rankings, or testimonials.
- Privacy and consent: collect and use personal data only with a lawful basis and consent where required. Respect PDPA (Singapore) and GDPR; honour unsubscribes and opt-outs immediately.
- Platform policy: follow each ad and search platform's current policies. When a rule changes often, flag it for a human to re-check before launch.
- Original or properly licensed content only. No plagiarism, no scraped articles, no AI-spun rewrites of competitors' work.
- Statistics and quotes must trace to a real source the reader can check.
