# Brand Voice Guidelines

## What this skill does

Converts 'be more human' into an operational guide: three or four voice traits, each demonstrated with before/after rewrites of the company's own copy.

## Inputs to collect

- Positioning and messaging framework
- Samples of current copy (best and worst)
- Founder/leadership voice samples if it's a founder-led brand

## Workflow

1. **Mine the real voice** - Collect where the brand already sounds like itself: founder posts, best emails, support replies customers loved. Voice guidelines codify what works; they don't invent a personality.
2. **Pick 3-4 traits with tension** - Each trait as a slider with a guardrail: 'Direct, but never blunt', 'Expert, but never jargon-first'. Traits without the 'but never' become caricatures.
3. **Write the example bank** - For each trait: two real before/after rewrites (headline, email opener, error message, social reply). Examples teach faster than definitions.
4. **Set tone flexes** - How the voice bends by context: crisis/apology, celebration, technical support, sales. Same voice, different register - with an example each.
5. **Add the mechanics** - Capitalisation, terminology list (words we use/don't), numbers and formatting conventions, and how AI-assisted drafts get voice-checked before use.

## Deliverable

- Voice guidelines: traits, sliders, example bank, tone flexes
- Terminology do/don't list
- Voice-check step for the content workflow

## Success metrics

| Metric | How to read it |
|--------|---------------|
| Voice audit pass rate | Sampled live assets scored against the guide quarterly |
| Edit distance on drafts | Reviewer changes per draft should fall as the guide beds in |
| Customer language echoes | Customers repeating your phrases back is the deepest signal |

## Quality checklist

- [ ] Every trait has real before/after examples
- [ ] Crisis tone defined before a crisis
- [ ] Guidelines usable by a new hire in one reading

## Guardrails

- Claims discipline: use only proof the user supplies or verifies. Never invent clients, results, rankings, or testimonials.
- Privacy and consent: collect and use personal data only with a lawful basis and consent where required. Respect PDPA (Singapore) and GDPR; honour unsubscribes and opt-outs immediately.
- Platform policy: follow each ad and search platform's current policies. When a rule changes often, flag it for a human to re-check before launch.
- Brand claims obey the same proof rules as performance claims. Aspiration is fine; fabrication is not.
