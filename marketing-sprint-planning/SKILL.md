# Marketing Sprint Planning (Agency)

## What this skill does

Applies sprint discipline to agency delivery without cargo-culting software rituals: commit to what capacity allows, finish before starting, show the work.

## Inputs to collect

- Team capacity (real hours by person, minus meetings and support load)
- Backlog of scoped client work with estimates
- Fixed commitments: reporting dates, campaign launches, approvals needed

## Workflow

1. **Cost the capacity honestly** - Available production hours after meetings, account management and the unexpected-work tax (reserve 15-20%). Sprint commitments are made against this number, not against hope.
2. **Pull, don't push, the work** - Select sprint items by client priority and deadline reality until capacity is full. Everything else is visibly next, which is how you say no without saying no.
3. **Set WIP limits** - Maximum concurrent items per person/stage (e.g. 2 in production, 3 in review). Blocked items surface in the daily check because the limit makes them visible.
4. **Run the mid-sprint check** - 15 minutes: what's blocked, what needs client input (chase it that day), what gets descoped by agreement if time runs out - agreed order of sacrifice in advance.
5. **Demo and retro** - End of sprint: show finished work to stakeholders, log what shipped vs committed, and take one process improvement into the next sprint. Retros that change nothing get skipped - make them change one thing.

## Deliverable

- Sprint plan with capacity maths shown
- WIP limits and blocked-item process
- Demo notes and one retro improvement per sprint

## Success metrics

| Metric | How to read it |
|--------|---------------|
| Commitment reliability | Shipped vs committed items per sprint - aim stable 80%+ |
| Cycle time per deliverable type | Falls as WIP limits bite |
| Client approval wait time | Tracked as a sprint input, raised with data when it's the bottleneck |

## Quality checklist

- [ ] Capacity calculation includes the unexpected-work reserve
- [ ] Sacrifice order agreed before it's needed
- [ ] Retro produces exactly one implemented change

## Guardrails

- Claims discipline: use only proof the user supplies or verifies. Never invent clients, results, rankings, or testimonials.
- Privacy and consent: collect and use personal data only with a lawful basis and consent where required. Respect PDPA (Singapore) and GDPR; honour unsubscribes and opt-outs immediately.
- Platform policy: follow each ad and search platform's current policies. When a rule changes often, flag it for a human to re-check before launch.
- Client data stays confidential and is used only for that client's work. Never reuse one client's data, results or creative for another client's pitch without written permission.
- Report client results honestly, including the misses. A client who discovers a hidden failure leaves; a client told early usually stays.
