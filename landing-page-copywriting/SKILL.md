# Landing Page Copywriting

## What this skill does

Produces page copy structured around the visitor's decision process - not the company's org chart - using research language instead of marketing adjectives.

## Inputs to collect

- Offer sheet and claims register
- Customer language: reviews, interviews, sales call phrases
- The traffic source and its promise (ad, email, search query)

## Workflow

1. **Lead with the visitor's outcome** - Headline states the outcome or the problem solved, in words from customer research. Subhead adds mechanism or proof. Company name is not a headline.
2. **Sequence by decision order** - What is it, why believe it, how it works, what it costs/asks, what happens next. Answer questions in the order visitors ask them.
3. **Place proof at the point of doubt** - Testimonials beside the claims they support, numbers near the price, credentials near the ask. Proof at the bottom under a logos strip is decoration.
4. **Handle the top objections** - Price, time, trust, fit - write the FAQ/objection section from real sales conversations, in plain answers, not deflections.
5. **Write one CTA, repeated consistently** - Same action, same words, at each decision point. Button copy says what happens next ('Get the audit', not 'Submit').

## Deliverable

- Full page copy: headline set, body sections, FAQ, CTAs
- Proof placement map against claims
- Headline variants for testing (3+)

## Success metrics

| Metric | How to read it |
|--------|---------------|
| Page conversion rate by traffic source | Copy judged per promise it must keep |
| Scroll depth to CTA sections | Shows where the argument loses people |
| Headline test results over time | Builds the voice-of-customer phrase bank |

## Quality checklist

- [ ] Headline understandable in 5 seconds by an outsider
- [ ] Every claim has proof on the page or in the register
- [ ] Only one primary action requested

## Guardrails

- Claims discipline: use only proof the user supplies or verifies. Never invent clients, results, rankings, or testimonials.
- Privacy and consent: collect and use personal data only with a lawful basis and consent where required. Respect PDPA (Singapore) and GDPR; honour unsubscribes and opt-outs immediately.
- Platform policy: follow each ad and search platform's current policies. When a rule changes often, flag it for a human to re-check before launch.
- No dark patterns: no fake countdowns, confirm-shaming, hidden costs or pre-ticked add-ons. Conversion bought with deception is refunded with interest in trust.
- Social proof must be real: genuine testimonials with permission, accurate numbers, no fabricated reviews or logos.
