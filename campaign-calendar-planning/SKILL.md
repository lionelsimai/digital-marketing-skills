# Campaign Calendar Planning

## What this skill does

Turns scattered campaign ideas into one calendar that respects production lead times, seasonal moments and team capacity - so campaigns launch finished, not rushed.

## Inputs to collect

- Fixed dates: launches, events, sales periods, seasonal peaks for the market
- Production capacity: who makes copy, creative, pages, email
- Last year's campaign results by period

## Workflow

1. **Anchor the fixed moments** - Place immovable dates first: product launches, industry events, retail peaks (e.g. 11.11, year-end), and company milestones.
2. **Set one theme per month** - Give each month a single campaign theme tied to a funnel stage. Always-on activity continues underneath; the theme is what gets produced and promoted.
3. **Work backwards for assets** - For each campaign, schedule assets by lead time: page, email, ads, creative. If the asset list cannot be produced in time, shrink the campaign, not the quality bar.
4. **Balance the mix** - Check the quarter has capture, nurture and conversion campaigns - not four launches and no follow-up.
5. **Add review slots** - Book a post-campaign review within 7 days of each campaign ending, with the metric it will be judged on decided now.

## Deliverable

- Quarter calendar: themes, dates, owners, asset deadlines
- Asset production list per campaign
- Review schedule with per-campaign success metrics

## Success metrics

| Metric | How to read it |
|--------|---------------|
| On-time launch rate | Campaigns that launched with all assets ready |
| Campaign revenue/pipeline vs target | By theme, to learn which themes earn repeat slots |
| Asset reuse rate | Campaign assets reused in always-on activity |

## Quality checklist

- [ ] No month carries more campaigns than the team can produce
- [ ] Every campaign has a pre-agreed success metric
- [ ] Seasonal moments reflect the actual market (e.g. SG retail calendar)

## Guardrails

- Claims discipline: use only proof the user supplies or verifies. Never invent clients, results, rankings, or testimonials.
- Privacy and consent: collect and use personal data only with a lawful basis and consent where required. Respect PDPA (Singapore) and GDPR; honour unsubscribes and opt-outs immediately.
- Platform policy: follow each ad and search platform's current policies. When a rule changes often, flag it for a human to re-check before launch.
- Strategy before channels: never recommend a channel or budget split without the offer, audience and unit economics in hand.
