# Rebrand Rollout Plan

## What this skill does

Prevents the classic rebrand mess - three logos live at once, broken links, confused customers - with a complete surface inventory and a sequenced switch.

## Inputs to collect

- New identity assets and guidelines
- Current brand asset inventory (site, ads, social, email, decks, partner materials)
- Announcement strategy and timing constraints

## Workflow

1. **Inventory every surface** - Systematically list where the old brand lives: website, templates, ad accounts, social profiles, email signatures, partner sites, printed stock, app listings. Assign an owner to each.
2. **Sequence by visibility and risk** - Owned high-visibility first (site, profiles), then templates and ads, then partner/third-party updates with lead time. Legal/regulatory names change on their own track with compliance.
3. **Protect the technical layer** - If domains or URLs change: redirect maps, analytics continuity, UTM and link updates, and search console change-of-address where applicable. Test every redirect.
4. **Write the customer communication** - What changed, what didn't (accounts, contracts, contacts), and why - in the brand voice, sent before or at the switch, not discovered by customers.
5. **Run the post-launch sweep** - Crawl and search for old-brand remnants at day 1, 7 and 30: screenshots, cached pages, partner listings, stray templates. Log and burn down the list.

## Deliverable

- Surface inventory with owners and switch dates
- Redirect and technical continuity map
- Customer communication draft and sweep schedule

## Success metrics

| Metric | How to read it |
|--------|---------------|
| Old-brand remnants found per sweep | Should approach zero by day 30 |
| Traffic/conversion continuity | No unexplained dips attributable to the switch |
| Partner surface update rate | Third parties updated within the agreed window |

## Quality checklist

- [ ] Every inventoried surface has an owner and a date
- [ ] Redirects tested individually, not assumed
- [ ] Customer comms sent before customers notice on their own

## Guardrails

- Claims discipline: use only proof the user supplies or verifies. Never invent clients, results, rankings, or testimonials.
- Privacy and consent: collect and use personal data only with a lawful basis and consent where required. Respect PDPA (Singapore) and GDPR; honour unsubscribes and opt-outs immediately.
- Platform policy: follow each ad and search platform's current policies. When a rule changes often, flag it for a human to re-check before launch.
- Brand claims obey the same proof rules as performance claims. Aspiration is fine; fabrication is not.
