# Competitor Digital Audit

## What this skill does

Produces an evidence-based view of how 3-5 competitors actually market - what they promise, where they spend, what they publish - and where the open ground is.

## Inputs to collect

- List of 3-5 direct competitors
- Access to public sources: sites, ad libraries, social profiles, review sites
- Your current positioning and offer

## Workflow

1. **Capture positioning** - Record each competitor's homepage promise, primary audience, entry offer and pricing posture, with screenshots and dates.
2. **Map channel presence** - Check ad libraries (Meta, Google, LinkedIn transparency), organic search visibility for shared keywords, and publishing cadence per channel.
3. **Analyse the offer ladder** - Note lead magnets, trials, webinars, guarantees and how aggressively each moves a prospect to a sales conversation.
4. **Mine customer language** - Read their reviews (and yours). Extract what customers praise and complain about - complaints are positioning openings.
5. **Decide, don't just describe** - End with 3 decisions: a claim to own, a channel to contest or avoid, and an offer gap to fill. An audit without decisions is trivia.

## Deliverable

- Competitor matrix: promise, audience, offers, channels, cadence
- Ad and content evidence pack (dated)
- Three positioning decisions with rationale

## Success metrics

| Metric | How to read it |
|--------|---------------|
| Share of search on shared keywords | Baseline now, re-check quarterly |
| Ad presence continuity | Weeks per quarter each competitor is actively advertising |
| Review sentiment gap | Your praised/complained themes vs theirs |

## Quality checklist

- [ ] Every claim sourced and dated
- [ ] Direct competitors only - not aspirational brands
- [ ] Ends in decisions, not just observations

## Guardrails

- Claims discipline: use only proof the user supplies or verifies. Never invent clients, results, rankings, or testimonials.
- Privacy and consent: collect and use personal data only with a lawful basis and consent where required. Respect PDPA (Singapore) and GDPR; honour unsubscribes and opt-outs immediately.
- Platform policy: follow each ad and search platform's current policies. When a rule changes often, flag it for a human to re-check before launch.
- Strategy before channels: never recommend a channel or budget split without the offer, audience and unit economics in hand.
- Use only public information. No fake enquiries, no scraping behind logins, no misrepresentation to extract pricing.
