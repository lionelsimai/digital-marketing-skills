# Abandon Recovery Flows

## What this skill does

Recovers revenue left on the table by people who showed real intent, using reminders that help (details, answers, proof) rather than pressure tricks.

## Inputs to collect

- Abandonment points and current recovery rate
- Product/offer details, shipping and returns facts
- Margin room for any incentive, and the policy on using it

## Workflow

1. **Segment the abandon type** - Cart vs checkout vs browse are different intents. Checkout abandons get speed; browse abandons get education and proof, later and lighter.
2. **Set the timing ladder** - First touch within 1-4 hours for cart/checkout while intent is warm, then 24h, then 48-72h. Cap the series at 3; desperation reads as spam.
3. **Handle the real objections** - Each email answers one abandonment cause: total cost surprise (show the full price breakdown), trust (guarantee, reviews that are real), questions (reply-to-a-human).
4. **Use incentives last, and honestly** - If margin allows, reserve any discount for the final email and for segments that need it. Never invent stock counts, countdowns or 'someone else is buying this'.
5. **Suppress correctly** - Purchasers exit instantly; anyone in a support conversation pauses. Test suppression with real scenarios, not assumptions.

## Deliverable

- Recovery flows per abandon type: timing, copy, rules
- Objection-to-email map
- Suppression and exit test record

## Success metrics

| Metric | How to read it |
|--------|---------------|
| Recovery rate per abandon type | Recovered orders / abandons, by series position |
| Revenue per recovery email sent | Watch it against unsubscribe cost |
| Unsubscribe/complaint rate on the series | The guardrail that keeps recovery honest |

## Quality checklist

- [ ] No fake scarcity, stock or social proof claims anywhere
- [ ] Purchasers suppressed in near-real-time
- [ ] Full price (incl. shipping) shown honestly in recovery emails

## Guardrails

- Claims discipline: use only proof the user supplies or verifies. Never invent clients, results, rankings, or testimonials.
- Privacy and consent: collect and use personal data only with a lawful basis and consent where required. Respect PDPA (Singapore) and GDPR; honour unsubscribes and opt-outs immediately.
- Platform policy: follow each ad and search platform's current policies. When a rule changes often, flag it for a human to re-check before launch.
- Permission-based sending only: no purchased or scraped lists, ever. Clear consent, honest sender identity, one-click unsubscribe in every email.
- Deliverability is trust: no deceptive subject lines, no hidden downsides, no dark patterns in preference or unsubscribe flows.
- Never fabricate urgency: no false countdowns, invented low-stock warnings or fake 'other shoppers' claims.
