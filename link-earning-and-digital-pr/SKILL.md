# Link Earning and Digital PR

## What this skill does

Plans link earning the defensible way: create something journalists and sites genuinely want to cite, then pitch it to the people who cover that topic.

## Inputs to collect

- Original data, research, tools or expert access the business can offer
- Target publications and journalists in the niche
- Existing link profile and competitor link gaps

## Workflow

1. **Choose the asset** - Pick a linkable asset with a news or utility hook: original survey/data, a free tool, a definitive resource, or data-backed commentary. No asset, no campaign.
2. **Build the target list** - List sites that already link to similar assets or cover the topic. Relevance beats domain metrics; 30 right targets beat 300 scraped ones.
3. **Craft the pitch** - One short personalised pitch per segment: the finding, why their readers care, the asset link, and an offer of exclusive data or comment.
4. **Follow up once, gracefully** - One polite follow-up. Log responses; a no is data about the asset, not a reason to nag.
5. **Report honestly** - Report links earned, coverage quality and what the asset cost. Also report what failed - it shapes the next asset.

## Deliverable

- Linkable asset brief and build spec
- Target list with pitch angles per segment
- Campaign report: outreach, responses, links earned

## Success metrics

| Metric | How to read it |
|--------|---------------|
| Relevant referring domains earned | Quality-weighted, not raw count |
| Pitch response and conversion rate | Diagnoses asset vs targeting problems |
| Assisted rankings on target clusters | Links should move the pages they support |

## Quality checklist

- [ ] Every target is editorially relevant
- [ ] Pitches disclose who is sending them and why
- [ ] No payment or exchange offered for links

## Guardrails

- Claims discipline: use only proof the user supplies or verifies. Never invent clients, results, rankings, or testimonials.
- Privacy and consent: collect and use personal data only with a lawful basis and consent where required. Respect PDPA (Singapore) and GDPR; honour unsubscribes and opt-outs immediately.
- Platform policy: follow each ad and search platform's current policies. When a rule changes often, flag it for a human to re-check before launch.
- White-hat only: no purchased links, link schemes, PBNs, keyword stuffing, cloaking or scraped/spun content. Earn coverage and links with assets worth citing.
- Never buy, exchange or automate links. If a placement requires payment, it is advertising - label and treat it as such, with proper link attributes.
